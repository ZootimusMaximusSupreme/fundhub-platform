## New information (15)

- 60% of online-lender borrowers said actual costs were higher than expected (vs 37% small banks, 32% large banks); online lender satisfaction 35% vs 76% credit unions and 64-65% banks; online lenders took ~29% of applications; online lenders had the lowest offer acceptance rate (84% vs 94% credit unions). — https://www.americascreditunions.org/node/25651 (America's Credit Unions, Curt Long (Chief Economist), March 31, 2026 - analysis of Federal Reserve 2025 Small Business Credit Survey / 2026 Report on Employer Firms)
- 42% of applicants got full financing (highest since 2021), 36% partial, 22% nothing; firms over $1M revenue had approval rates nearly twice those under $100K; applicants who applied to multiple lender types reported higher full-approval rates; most common denial reason was insufficient collateral or credit history. — https://beancount.io/blog/2026/07/20/fed-small-business-credit-survey-loan-approval-denial-rates-guide (Beancount.io guide (July 20, 2026) summarizing Federal Reserve Banks, 2026 Report on Employer Firms (released March 3, 2026; 6,500+ employer firms))
- SOP 50 10 8 (effective June 1, 2025) reinstated a personal-resources test under 'credit elsewhere', requires lenders to document specific reasons no other credit is available, cut the 7(a) Small Loan maximum from $500K to $350K, and raised the minimum SBSS score from 155 to 165. — https://www.everycrsreport.com/reports/IN12549.html (Congressional Research Service, 'Changes to Small Business Administration (SBA) Business Loan Program Policies in Early 2025' (IN12549), April 28, 2025)
- Search results reported that FY2026 9-month SBA 7(a) approvals fell 33.4% by count and 20.9% by dollars, with the drop concentrated in loans under $500K; average 7(a) loan size rose to $477,600. Coleman Report argues the government shutdown distorts the year-over-year comparison. — https://www.spglobal.com/market-intelligence/en/news-insights/articles/2026/5/sba-7-a-lending-rebounds-in-q1-2026-as-banks-adjust-to-policy-changes-101394123 (S&P Global Market Intelligence, May 2026 (with Coleman Report coverage); exact attribution of figures NOT confirmed - search summary only)
- Combined defaults at major MCA providers reportedly reached $2.22B in 2024, up 59% from $1.40B in 2023; owners stacking several advances reportedly default at 3-5x the rate of single-advance owners; Bloomberg Law counted 230+ bankruptcy filings listing MCA debt, a peak. — https://www.barchart.com/story/news/37072113/merchant-cash-advance-defaults-surge-59-to-22-billion-as-businesses-turn-to-reverse-consolidation (Barchart syndicated story (likely a press release; date and author not confirmed because the page was blank); related coverage from Bloomberg Government/Bloomberg Law on MCAs in small-business bankruptcies)

- The SBA stopped using the FICO SBSS score for 7(a) small loans as of March 1, 2026. 7(a) loans under $350K now need a debt service coverage ratio of at least 1.1:1 on historical and/or projected cash flow, analysed the same way as similar non-SBA commercial loans. Internal models may not rely solely on consumer credit scores. — https://www.naggl.org/sba-notice-formally-announcing-sunsetting-of-sbss-scoring-and-providing-new-underwriting-requirements-for-7a-small-loans/ (NAGGL, on SBA Procedural Notice 5000-875701 (issued January 16, 2026; effective March 1, 2026); also covered by Experian Business Information blog, July 22, 2026)
