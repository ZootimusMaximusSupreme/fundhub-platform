# Capital Blueprint — what it is, in Chris's words (2026-10-14)

Chris said this in a Claude chat on 2026-10-14 so ads stop getting it wrong. Use this before
`marketing/offers/fundhub-offers-2026-10-10.md` when writing Blueprint copy.

## What it is

A system that takes anyone, wherever they are, down the shortest path of least resistance to the
maximum amount of funding.

- A file usually has 13 optimization points that need to be optimized.
- There are more things you can do while you wait for your credit to be optimized, because it is a
  process.
- A process done out of order does not work, or it gets a weaker result.
- So instead of a course that teaches the process, the Blueprint makes you go through the process to
  get what you want.

## What it walks you through

- Which banks to open accounts with
- Where to deposit money
- Which cards to pay down
- Which items need to be removed from the file
- Which items need to be added to the file
- Timelines for when you will be fundable
- How your fundability improves as you optimize
- How to optimize your business data points so you don't look sketchy to a bank
- Other features beyond these

It does not only show you. It walks you through doing all of it, so you learn by taking action.

## Support

- Support and accountability are built into the system.
- You can touch base with an advisor who answers your questions and makes sure you are moving the
  right way.
- In client-facing copy, say "advisor". Never say "CSM".
- The goal is to get the client fundable.

## Likely upsell (not final)

When a Blueprint client becomes fundable, a CSM reaches out and upsells them to fund with Fundhub
instead of on their own. Chris: "I don't know." Related ideas: `marketing/offers/data-points-ideas-2026-10-14.md`.

## What Blueprint ads leave out (owner-set 2026-10-15)

- No application sequences, no bank order, and no "what to put on each application" in Blueprint ads.
- Nothing about what happens after funding (no "it tells you when you're ready to go again").
- Reason (Chris): if a $10,000 product gives them everything and lets them use the system forever,
  they never need Fundhub again, and that cuts lifetime value. Leave something out so they keep
  working with Fundhub (the funding itself, or the data points).
