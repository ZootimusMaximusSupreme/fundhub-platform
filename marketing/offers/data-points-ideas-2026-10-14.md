# Data points — offer ideas (2026-10-14)

Chris said these out loud in a Claude chat on 2026-10-14 and asked that they not get lost. **These are
ideas, not decisions.** His words: "there's no need for more offers. But these are all just ideas."

## What people value most

- The data points are the most valuable thing to people.
- Data points means: the right banks, the right position (order), what to put on the applications,
  and what order to apply in.
- Fundhub's systems stay in constant contact with lenders across the US, so the data points stay
  current. The systems keep getting better.
- On either offer (funding or Capital Blueprint), a client does not get the system. They get what the
  system shows for their own one-time funding round.

## Ideas

1. **Bank-order upsell inside the Capital Blueprint.** The client works the Blueprint until their file
   reaches a funding point. Then they pay a fee of about $1,000 and get the banks, in the right order,
   to apply at. Or they let Fundhub run the funding for them. Chris: "That'd actually be a lot
   smoother."
2. **Data points subscription.** About $2,000 to $3,000 a month for data points that stay updated, for
   people who want to run their own funding company.
3. **White label.** The same people can white label with Fundhub instead.
4. **Teach them to run their own company.** Sell how to run their own funding company.
