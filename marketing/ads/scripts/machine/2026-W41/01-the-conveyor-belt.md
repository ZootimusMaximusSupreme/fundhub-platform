The inquiries from your FIRST round of applications cut how much funding your next round gets you, and whoever ran your last applications left them sitting there.

There's one step that happens between EVERY round, and it decides how many rounds you get ↑

- Soft pull on all three bureaus first, zero impact on your score
- Your file gets matched against 30 to 50 lenders that fit it
- Old hard inquiries get disputed before a single application goes out
- First round goes to the lenders that fit your file
- Then we go after the new inquiries those banks just created
- Next batch goes in with a CLEAN file
- Up to 12 rounds in one funding sequence
- A decade doing this, hundreds of files, thousands of data points

That step is clearing the new inquiries between rounds, so EVERY round goes in on a clean file and goes after the maximum amount of funding your file can get you.
