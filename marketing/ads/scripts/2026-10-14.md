## The structure (from a Matt Shiver ad Chris screenshotted)

Short direct offer ad:

1. Hook — call out the avatar + their problem or goal
2. Promise with risk reversal
3. Qualify (add emotion)
4. Proof
5. CTA
6. Risk reversal again

None of the 11 Direct Book ads in `book-a-call-final-2026-10-03.md` used this structure. Closest were
Ad 15 (avatar call-out and an emotional yes/no qualify) and Ad 14 (the only risk-reversal line).

## Owner calls made in this chat

- Two offers get ads. Everyone who fits neither falls into credit optimization on the call.
- **Direct Book (funding):** business owners with 700+, and 780+ for the premium lane. Do not mention
  income. Banks don't verify it.
- **Capital Blueprint ($5,000 to $10,000):** 600+, maybe some negative items or high balances, wants
  the maximum amount of funding as fast as possible, would rather work their own file than buy a
  course. Some 700+ people fit here too when they want to do it themselves (the bank list, the
  process).
- **Funding risk reversal = the guarantees on fundhub.ai** (`public/index.html`): advisor starts
  within 24 business hours; applications go out within 72 business hours; if you qualify and we don't
  secure funding for you, you don't pay.
- **Capital Blueprint guarantee (Chris, 22:39):** "We guarantee this is the last tool you use to learn
  the funding game." The soft pull still has zero impact on the score.
- The most valuable thing to people is the data points: the right banks, what to put on each
  application, and the order to apply in. Fundhub's systems stay in constant contact with lenders
  across the US, so the data stays current. Clients see what the system shows for their own funding
  round, not the system itself.
