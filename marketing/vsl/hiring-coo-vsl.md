# Hiring VSL (COO) — bullet cues

Owner-set 2026-10-13. For the COO hiring funnel at fundhub.ai/join/coo/. Chris
freestyles from these cues on camera, talking straight to the operator he wants
to hire.

Film in 4K. This is a VSL, not an ad. The finished file goes to
`public/funnel/hiring-coo-vsl.mp4`; the page plays it as soon as the file is
there and shows a placeholder until then.

Aim for 4 to 6 minutes.

## Cues

1. I'm hiring a COO to run Fundhub's operations with me.
2. Who I am: I learned credit card stacking ten years ago, I've funded a little over $1,000,000 for my own companies, and I've invested that capital in cash-flowing companies for ten years.
3. Where the company is today: [monthly revenue, clients funded — fill in before filming]. The average client gets $100,000 to $300,000 in funding.
4. The team is already in place: a sales manager, closers, a client success manager, a funding advisor, a credit optimization specialist, an affiliate program and content creators.
5. The systems are already built: our own underwriting software, UnderwriteIQ, a CRM and client portal, an hourly check on every money path, and internal AI tools for marketing and operations.
6. What I do best is build the product, the offers and the marketing. I need someone who makes sure every process runs end to end, every week.
7. What you own: the team, the numbers, the weekly meetings and the hiring. You report to me.
8. Your first 90 days: learn every seat and every number, run a weekly KPI meeting with a scorecard for every seat, hire and onboard closers and CSMs, and give every step from ad click to funded client an owner and a number.
9. We're scaling paid acquisition, so your job is to make sure the company handles the growth without dropping a client.
10. Comp: [base, plus equity or profit share — fill in before filming].
11. The application takes about 5 minutes. You'll add your LinkedIn, a founder or investor I can call, and a 90-second Loom.
12. Strong fits pick a time with me right after they apply. Click Apply for the COO Seat below.
