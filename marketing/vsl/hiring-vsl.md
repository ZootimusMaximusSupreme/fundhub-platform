## Cues

6. Your sales manager coaches you and holds you to clear numbers. Our CRM and internal AI tools do the busywork, so your time goes to calls.
7. Setter: you work inbound leads fast and book qualified calls for the closers.
8. Closer: you run the booked calls, find the right path for each person, and close.
9. CSM: you own each client after the sale, keep their steps moving, and get them ready to fund.
10. Pay for each seat: [base / commission / OTE — fill in before filming].
11. You'll do well here if you take coaching, know your numbers every week, and show up to every huddle.
12. The application takes about 5 minutes. You'll add your LinkedIn, a reference we can call, and a 90-second Loom.
13. The best fits pick an interview time with our sales manager right after they apply. Click Apply Now below.
