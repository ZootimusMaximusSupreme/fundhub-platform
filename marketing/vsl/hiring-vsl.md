6. Closer: you run the booked calls, find the right path for each person, and close.
7. CSM: you own each client after the sale, keep their steps moving, and get them ready to fund.
8. COO: you run the operating rhythm — the team, the numbers, the weekly meetings. You interview with me.
9. Pay for each seat: [base / commission / OTE — fill in before filming].

12. The best fits pick an interview time with our sales manager right after they apply. Click Apply Now below.
