TC-02 / Your report

This sample scores over 700 at every bureau, and cards at 88% used still cut its funding.

  • Your advisor pulls all three bureaus with you on the line and puts the report on one shared screen.
  • This sample's four cards owe $37,100 on $42,000 of limits, which is 88% of the limits in use.
  • Three scores run across the top of the report once it's pulled. This sample reads 702, 711 and 706, which is GOOD credit at all three bureaus.
  • Under the scores sit the accounts. This file has four credit cards, and together those cards owe $37,100 against $42,000 of combined limits.
  • That's 88% of the limits in use. UnderwriteIQ reads 88% as CRITICAL, and that one reading cuts what this file qualifies for, good scores and all.
  • Card use is part of what sets how much you qualify for. A file can score well and still get held back by balances like these.
  • A bank can pull any one of the three bureaus. Your advisor pulls all THREE and checks that your name, address and accounts match on each one.
  • You end up looking at the same report the bank reads, and every choice after this point gets made on facts from your own file.
