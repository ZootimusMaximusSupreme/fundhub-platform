Pre-underwriting

PU-01 / The red flag

When a bank denies good credit, the reason was on the credit report first.

If nobody reads that line before you apply, the bank finds the red flag before you do.

PU-02 / The reasons

The top of your credit report lists the reasons a bank says yes or no.

The letter a bank sends after a no gives those same reasons.

PU-03 / The cost

A no from a bank still costs you a hard inquiry.

Almost every application you send is a hard pull. When there are too many, banks say no on their own.

PU-04 / Pre-underwriting

We review your file the way a lender does, before a single application goes out.

That's pre-underwriting, and we do it with the same models the banks use.

PU-05 / The right banks

Your applications go only to the banks that fit your file.

We check your file against each bank first, so you only apply where your file makes sense.

PU-06 / The guarantee

Applications go out within 72 business hours.

That's our guarantee, and it's in your contract. That typically means approvals come very shortly after.

PU-07 / Your call

On your call, we review your file with you, the way a lender does.

You leave knowing how much you qualify for based on where you're at right now.
