5. BA-05 / The businesses — Why does the LLC step sit early on your checklist when the business comes later?

The LLC step sits early on your checklist because the engine weighs a company's age, and that age counts from the date the state records your filing.

Under 12 months, the engine halves the business funding it reads. Past 24 months, it doubles it.

Scroll to the business steps. File your LLC online with the Secretary of State, then send us the confirmation you get back.

That confirmation is what closes the step, because the app has no line into the state's records. Then you open business checking in the LLC's name.

On this sample both steps are done and dated, and both are tagged you. After that, you add the business under Money, then Fundability.
