Credit Partner

1. CP-01 / A second file — A willing partner adds a second file to review alongside your own.

Your own credit file shapes the funding plan we build for you. One partner file is part of the Blueprint, and it goes to work before your file is ready.

The reason is simple. Every amount on your plan comes off one person's report. Right now your plan holds only what your own report reaches.

Take this SAMPLE owner. The cards run 86 percent used, one collection sits on two bureaus, and the engine says this file qualifies for $5,280 today.

When your own report keeps your funding low, the move is a spouse or business partner with GOOD credit. With their own consent, their file gives us a second path to review while you work on yours.

2. CP-02 / Their file — Your partner signs before a soft pull reads their file.

The first review is a soft pull, so it does not lower your partner's score. Nothing on their file gets read until THEY sign their own form. Later funding applications need their own consent and can create hard inquiries.

On the Capital Blueprint, one spouse or business partner joins your file as a second applicant. It's written into the offer, and that partner file is included.

The form spells it out, word for word: the soft pull doesn't lower their score, and no lender sees it as an application for credit.

Your partner stays in control of their permission. The form says they withdraw it in writing whenever they choose, and it covers one pull only.

Once they sign, the Blueprint builds their checklist from their own report, so every card and every item on their list belongs to their file.

Their list holds the same kinds of steps yours does, like a paydown target worked out from their own card limit, so their part of the work is clear.

Both files move at the same TIME. You work your steps, and your partner works theirs, on the same plan.

3. CP-03 / The math — The planning total adds two file estimates; each lender decides the actual amount.

Your total is your amount plus your partner's amount, and each of those comes from its own file. That's the whole MATH, and it's simple.

Each amount is our estimate from that person's report as it reads today. Your report counts for you, and theirs counts for them.

Then your plan adds the two together and shows ONE total. That total is what the two of you are working toward, step by step.

The sample owner from the start, the $5,280 file, has a spouse with three seasoned cards used about 6 percent and a car loan paid in full.

The engine reads the partner's best card, a $25,000 line open since 2013, at five and a half times its limit. It reads the paid-off $32,000 car loan at three times.

Run through the engine on its own, the partner's file comes back at $233,500. The credit partner rule adds the two, for a plan total of $238,780.

That's 45 TIMES what the owner's file reaches alone. Counted together, the two files give this couple a plan built on the stronger report too.

And when the files are ready, you take that total to the banks yourself. Each bank makes its own decision on each application.

4. CP-04 / My circle — I fund my own deals with a circle of partners, and your credit partner starts yours.

Everyone should build a funding circle, and I build mine all the time. It's how I fund my own deals, and it's how yours starts.

My funding circle is my inner group of partners, and every one of them helps fund my deals. I still fund myself on top of that.

Every partner in my circle brings their own credit file to the deals we fund TOGETHER, and every one of those files gets read on its own.

The Blueprint reads your partner's file separately and shows both estimates on one plan. That is a planning view, not a bank approval.

On the Blueprint, the FIRST person in your circle is your credit partner, and that one included file is where your circle starts.

A partner file gives you a second route to review while your own file gets ready. The partner has to understand and authorize any credit obligation in their name.

Over time, that's what a circle gets your business. More than one credit file stands behind the deals you take on, starting with your partner's file.

5. CP-05 / Your call — Your partner is welcome on your strategy call, where you both see your real file.

The partner file starts once you join the Blueprint and your partner signs their form, so it goes to work before your own file is ready.

And that person is welcome on your strategy CALL. If you make money decisions with someone, they hear the plan at the same moment you do.

When they hear it with you, nobody has to explain the plan twice, and the two of you decide with the same facts in front of you.

On the call, a soft pull reads your file while you're both sitting there. It leaves your score alone, and the banks never see it on your report.

Then you both see how much you qualify for based on where you're at right now, and which step on your file comes FIRST.

The Fundhub team and an AI coach stay with you through every step on the Blueprint, so you always know the next move on your file.

When the files are ready, you review the offers available to each person. Each lender makes its own decision, and each applicant signs for their own obligation.
