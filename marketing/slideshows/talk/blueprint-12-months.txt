Blueprint, 12 Months

1. BP-01 / The next step — When nobody is on your next step, the plan stalls and your file stays the same.

When nobody is on your next step, the plan stalls. And when the plan stalls, your file stays exactly the same, month after month, however good that plan was.

A real study shows how often that happens. Across 221 online courses, about seven out of eight people who signed up never FINISHED.

A plan changes your funding only when its steps get done, in order. Until then, your file reads to a bank exactly the way it reads today.

This sample checklist is six months in. Every letter step on it is checked off and tagged YOU, and the stamp under it marks the screen as a simulation.

A late step gets three reminders on the Blueprint. What happens after the third one is the part that keeps your file moving.

2. BP-02 / In writing — Your Blueprint agreement includes a file-based plan and 12 months of follow-up.

A written plan only starts the work. Your file changes during the year after it, as the steps get done, and the Blueprint covers that WHOLE year.

This is the actual agreement, section 1.1. It calls the Blueprint a twelve-month planning and ACCOUNTABILITY program. That last word is the one that counts for you.

We prepare your written Blueprint from the information you give us. Then a checklist in your portal walks you through the plan, one step at a time.

An AI accountability coach keeps track of your progress and follows up with you. When something needs a real person, the Fundhub team handles it, including getting you ready for funding.

That means for twelve months your plan lives in your portal, it's in your agreement, and your next step has a coach on it and a team behind it.

3. BP-03 / Letters in the pack — The bureau's 30 to 45 days to answer your letters start on the day you mail them.

An item you can dispute reads the same way to a bank every month it sits on your report, and that's what a stalled letter step COSTS you.

While your letters wait in the pack, the bank keeps seeing that same item, and how much you qualify for stays right where it is.

The bureau's answer is due 30 to 45 days after your letters go out, and that time only STARTS on the day you mail them.

Every week the letters sit on your desk adds a week before any answer comes back. The item stays on your report the whole time.

That's why mailing sits near the top of your checklist, right after your card steps. It has a due date, and the mailing proof comes right behind it.

4. BP-04 / When you fall behind — What happens when the business gets busy and a step on your checklist slips?

What happens when the business gets busy and a step slips? This part matters most, because a late step is exactly where a file stops moving.

You get a text on the day the step is due. If it's still open, an email follows, and after that a second text.

If it's STILL open after those three, a person on the Fundhub team steps in, because by then a stuck step needs a real conversation.

Your AI coach stays on that same open step the whole time, so the follow-up never depends on you remembering it.

That gives you follow-up when business gets busy. Your steps still need your time.

5. BP-05 / Your part, our part — You do the card, letter, and business steps. We build the plan and follow up.

Your part is to do the steps and send proof. We build the plan, keep the checklist current, and follow up with you.

On the left is your side. You pay your cards down to their targets, you hold off on new credit, and you mail your letters and upload the proof.

When it's time for the business, you file the LLC and open business checking in its name. Those steps are YOURS, because it's your file and your company.

On the right is ours. We write your plan, build the checklist from your report, and rebuild your letters every time new credit information comes in.

Your coach checks in on whatever step is open, and our team answers what you ask and preps you for funding. FinanceOS keeps every account, card and due date on one page.

The first step is clear, and the team helps you work through it. Your part still takes time and proof.

6. BP-06 / File ready — The engine tells you when your file is ready, and then you fund yourself.

The day the engine calls your file ready, YOU are the one who funds off it, and the Fundhub team gets you prepped for that first.

Someone from our team calls you about funding. Funding has its own agreement, apart from the Blueprint, since the Blueprint's job is to get your file ready.

By then you've worked every step on your own file, so you know EXACTLY what moved it and why it moved.

The coach and team follow up while you complete the card, letter, and business steps on your file.

Your access runs twelve months from the day your payment clears. It ends there, with no automatic renewal and no charge after it unless you choose to renew.

7. BP-07 / Your call — Your strategy call turns each item on your report into a step for your year.

Your strategy call turns each item on your report into a step for your year. You see every one of those steps on your own file.

We bring up your file during the call with a soft pull. Your score stays put, and the report we pull is yours to keep either way.

A high card on your report becomes a paydown step, and an item you can dispute becomes letters you mail. The picture shows exactly that.

Next to each step you see whose part it is, yours or our team's, so the whole year is laid out for you before it starts.

Use a computer for the call if you can, so your file and every step on it are big enough to read.
