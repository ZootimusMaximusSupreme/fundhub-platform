1. MX-01 / One card — Our model starts with your personal file and a seasoned card before checking each business.

Your personal file sets what every company you own qualifies for. It does that through ONE card: your highest-limit card that's been open two years or more.

Which card that is, its limit and how long it's been open are all printed on your report right now, so the answer is already sitting on your file.

The picture shows how our system picks it. A newer card with a BIGGER limit gets passed over, because it hasn't been open two years yet.

Once a card qualifies, our system sizes each of your companies off that one card. The same rule let one personal file back four of my own companies.

Every company you add later gets sized off that same card, so the work you put into one personal file pays you back on each of them.

Every plan we build starts on your personal file, because that file sizes everything after it, all the way to the last company you add.

2. MX-02 / The order — Your personal file gets primed first, before any company you own applies for credit.

Your personal file goes first for one reason. Every company you own gets its business amount from that one card, once your personal file is ready.

With no card like that on your file, no company gets a business amount at all, however old the company is or however much it brings in.

Our system walks it in a set order: prime your personal file, send out your personal funding, then business one applies, then business two.

The picture shows the why. Business one and business two each get sized off the SAME card, and each one is read by itself, never lumped into one score.

And the applying never closes out. It keeps going round after round, and a business round can bring personal funding right along with it.

3. MX-03 / Company age — A company's age changes its amount: half in year one, double once it's two years old.

An idle company costs you real money. Each company you own applies for its own business credit, and our system reads each one by how OLD it is.

This is the companies screen from a Fundhub call. It shows personal, business one and business two, plus a button that adds one more company to the picture.

Age sets the amount. A company counts at half your personal card funding in its first year, the full amount in year two, and DOUBLE after two years.

That's the line marked on the screen. A company you formed two or more years ago already counts at DOUBLE the day it applies.

An older company may have options worth reviewing. We check its real file, revenue, and use for credit first.

More than one real business can be reviewed over time. Each needs its own file, revenue, and reason for credit.

4. MX-04 / Growth on credit — Business credit can give a company room for planned growth when the payment fits.

Once we review your personal and business files, we can look at credit for a specific growth plan.

Each company has its own lender decision. Add a business for a real business reason, then review whether credit fits.

Keep enough cash for the business, and use credit only when the plan can repay it.

The picture shows the whole setup: one primed personal file, each company with its own credit, your growth on top, and your savings off to the side.

That is the plan: compare each business's options, costs, and ability to repay before you use credit.

6. MX-06 / Where you start — Your strategy call reads your file live, that card included, and shows where you start.

On your strategy call, YOUR whole file gets read with you, that one card included, and the soft pull leaves your score where it is.

You see how much you qualify for based on where you're at right now. That amount is where your funding sequence STARTS, and the bank still makes its own decision.

The timeline shows what comes after that. Your funding sequence runs about six rounds of applications, and the new inquiries get disputed after each one.

After that sequence, we review the updated file before deciding whether another one fits. Any company you've opened in the meantime applies with its own business credit.

The call gives you the starting point and the plan after it: your personal file first, then each company, then the next funding sequence.

You can see the plan and ask what each offer costs before you decide. If the maximum amount of funding is what you want, the call is where you see YOUR path to it.
