Fundhub, The Money Talk

1. MY-01 / Tight cash — When cash is tight, we first check whether the plan is worth its price to you.

When cash is tight, your call is where it gets worked out. Your advisor works through a SET order of ways to start, one at a time.

Before money comes up at all, your advisor settles something that keeps you off the wrong plan. How you start depends on it, and it comes first.

If you believe in the plan, we can look at the real payment choices. If those do not fit, we can discuss a smaller service.

On the Capital Blueprint, you can pick your own payment dates, pay half now and the rest on payday, or split it into three payments starting today.

When the full plan is too much today, the order ends with a smaller place to start, at its REGULAR price, written into its agreement before you pay.

If money is the worry, bring it up on the call. We can compare the price with your goal and your actual budget.

2. MY-02 / How you pay — A loan or a big new card balance lands on the same file the banks are about to read.

The way you pay for this lands on the same credit file you're trying to get FUNDED on, and that file is about to be read by the banks.

Financing is only for the Capital Blueprint, through a lender. Funding and credit optimization get paid in cash, and your advisor tells you which applies to your path.

Seeing your financing options takes a soft check, so your score holds still. Accepting the loan takes a HARD pull, and that pull shows on your report.

A card works the same way. A big charge makes your card use climb, and since card use helps set what you qualify for, that charge costs you funding.

With financing, the rate and the monthly payment come from the lender, based on your file. You see both on screen, with your advisor still on, before you accept.

We read the lender terms and your file together before you choose a payment method.

3. MY-03 / Paid twice — Paying before anyone reads your file costs you twice: the fee and the hard pulls it buys.

Paying a big fee before anyone reads your file costs you TWICE. You pay the fee, and that money buys applications your file was never ready for.

Those applications go to banks your file doesn't fit. Each one is a hard pull, and every pull stays on your report for the next bank to read.

Your file comes FIRST on your call. After that you hear the fee in dollars, and the two of you go through the agreement on screen before any money moves.

Worried a company disappears once it's paid? After you pay, your portal opens with your next step already waiting, and the Fundhub team stays reachable the whole way.

Every promise sits in the agreement in writing, and you read it with your advisor before you pay, so nothing depends on something only said out loud.

The money you put down pays for a plan built on your own report, and you know exactly what it covers before it ever leaves your account.

4. MY-04 / How the fee works — For funding, your deposit counts dollar for dollar against a fee set by your approvals.

Section two of the funding agreement lays out the fee from start to finish. Your deposit counts toward the success fee, DOLLAR for dollar, one time.

The marked words say it plainly: credited once, dollar for dollar. You never pay that money twice, and it's written into the agreement you sign.

The fee base is your confirmed approved limits and amounts, so the fee follows what the banks actually APPROVE. It's figured round by round, with the record behind it.

Before a deposit is ever taken, you see how much you qualify for based on where you're at right now. UnderwriteIQ reads it off your own file.

That software was built from what we learned on hundreds of client files. Fundhub only takes a funding deposit after it confirms you qualify.

The rest comes due as that approved credit reaches you. The fee is based on approved access, including unused lines. Make sure the cost fits your plan before you accept.

You can pay that fee from a separate account. If a loan is approved and its terms fit, you can choose to use part of those proceeds. You do not have to withdraw cash against a card to pay us.

5. MY-05 / Soft pull form — The form you sign puts it in writing that the pull is soft and your score stays put.

Your report gets pulled before any talk about money, and the form you sign spells out exactly what that pull does to your file.

Line one of that form says it's a SOFT pull. It doesn't lower your score, and lenders never treat it as an application for credit.

The soft pull is visible to you alone. You find out what your file supports today WITHOUT leaving a mark for the next bank to read.

That pull is the only charge before any option or price comes up. Every option gets walked through after your file is read, never before it.

What you qualify for gets decided later, by the lender. That's the whole reason we review your file the way a lender does, before a single bank sees it.

You keep the reports whatever you decide, and your score sits right where it was before the call. It's a SAFE way to see what your file gets you.

6. MY-06 / Plan first — Set the cost aside for a second: does this plan fit your file?

The thing your advisor settles before money is the plan. With the cost set aside, does this plan fit YOUR file? Your answer steers the rest of the call.

If your answer is no, the plan gets fixed right there, before a price ever comes up, and the fixed plan is the only one you're asked to pay for.

If the plan fits, we can answer the cost question directly. That's when your advisor goes down the order, one way to start at a time.

That order gives you a clear choice on the call. By the time it comes up, you've seen your report, and you already know the plan is the RIGHT one.

Bring your real situation to the call, cash included, because saying it out loud is how your advisor finds the way to start that fits you.

Your file and goal shape the plan. Then we look at its real price and payment terms together.
