3. AY-03 / One bureau — Three applications on your own can all land on the one bureau the next bank reads.

Lenders choose which bureau or bureaus they read. Without checking first, you do not know where each inquiry will land. Three banks that all read Experian put three fresh pulls on ONE report.

The next bank that reads Experian finds all three close together and weighs them with the rest of the file.

Which bureau each bank reads decides where every pull lands, and on your own, that part happens blind.

On a plan, we check which bureaus lenders use, choose banks that fit your file, and set the order before you authorize applications.

When a bureau shows a fresh pull, we review that report before the next application and dispute an inquiry only if the facts support it.

Both sides start with the same file and the same score, and on a plan, no bureau collects a stack of fresh pulls at once.

On your report, a soft pull is visible to YOU and nobody else. The next bank that looks at your file won't find it there.
