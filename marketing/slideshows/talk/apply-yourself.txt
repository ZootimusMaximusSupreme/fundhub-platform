Does reading your file first cost you an inquiry too? Here's the form you sign before anyone looks, and line one answers it.





It says this is a soft pull. It doesn't lower your score, and no lender counts it as an application for credit.





On your report, a soft pull is visible to YOU and nobody else. The next bank that looks at your file won't find it there.





A hard pull needs your separate consent, written into the agreement. It only happens when an application you approved actually goes out.





That way you find out what's holding your file back, and what gets fixed first, without adding a single inquiry to the count.





That's the order the whole plan runs on. Your file gets read with no hard pull, and only then does anything go to a bank.
