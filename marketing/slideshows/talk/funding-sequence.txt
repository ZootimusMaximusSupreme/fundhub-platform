What happens when five applications go out the same afternoon? Each one drops a HARD inquiry on your report, and every bank can see all of them.





Each inquiry shows who pulled your file and on what date. So the next bank knows how many went out, and how recently they went.





When a bank sees that many fresh pulls at once, it starts declining. Rule three on your lender list is there to stop that from happening to you.





So one application goes out, and we WAIT for that bank's answer before the next one goes anywhere. The bank's decision sets the pace.





For you, every bank in the round reads your file with as few new pulls on it as possible, and that protects every application behind it.

So which banks make your list? Our system builds it from your file in three bands, and it reads top to bottom in that exact ORDER.





National banks come first. Next come the local banks in your home state, and then the local banks in the state where your business is set up.





Live in one state and run the business from another? You get BOTH sets of local banks, and a bank that covers both states only shows up once.





When a bureau already shows a fresh inquiry, any bank that pulls that bureau gets held back. That keeps your pulls SPREAD across all three bureaus.





The banks and the order are our job, and the yes or no belongs to the bank. That's why every bank on your list is one your file makes sense for.

Once a sequence wraps up, our system marks the day your file can start the next one. The Fundhub team gets that day too, so nobody has to remember it.
