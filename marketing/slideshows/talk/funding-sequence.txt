Funding Sequence

1. FS-01 / The order — Whichever bank sees your file first changes what every bank after it sees.

Whichever bank sees your file first changes what every bank after it sees. That's why the ORDER your applications go out in sets how much funding you end up with.

Your own file already decides what has to happen before the first one goes out. This is section three of the lender list Fundhub builds for each client.

It lays out five rules. The card balance gets fixed first, the lowest score floor goes first, and only ONE application goes out at a time.

Then you work up the list, and personal goes before business. Flip that order around, and the later banks see the earlier applications on your file.

Each of those denials still drops a hard inquiry on your report, and whichever bank comes next has all of them in front of it when it makes its call.

Sent in the wrong order, a good file can collect fresh inquiries before the next bank makes its decision. That's why we set the order from your file first.

2. FS-02 / Personal first — Your personal file sets the card funding, and a company two years or older counts double.

Underneath that order sits one rule inside our system. Every business amount starts from the card funding your PERSONAL file supports.

A stronger personal file raises the amount for every business you add, so nothing else gets worked until your personal file is READY.

Then each business gets counted by its age. A company two years or older counts at DOUBLE your personal card funding, and one under a year counts at half.

After that, every company is judged by itself. If you don't have a company yet, your path runs from personal funding straight to the banks.

Every company you add later gets figured from that same personal file. So the work you put into it now shows up again in each one of them.

3. FS-03 / One at a time — What happens when five applications land on your report the same afternoon?

Five applications going out the same afternoon means five HARD inquiries on your report, and every bank after them can see all five.

Each inquiry shows who pulled your file and on what date. So the next bank knows how many went out, and how recently they went.

When a bank sees that many fresh pulls at once, it starts declining. Rule three on your lender list is there to stop that from happening to you.

With us, one application goes out, and we WAIT for that bank's answer before the next one goes anywhere. The bank's decision sets the pace.

Every bank in the round reads your file with as few new pulls on it as possible, and that protects every application behind it.

4. FS-04 / Your banks — Your list holds national banks plus local banks where you live and where your business is.

Which banks make your list? Our system builds it from your file, out of three groups of banks.

National banks are one group. The local banks in your home state are the second, and the local banks in the state where your business is set up are the third.

Live in one state and run the business from another? You get BOTH sets of local banks, and a bank that covers both states only shows up once.

A bureau with a fresh inquiry on it gets protected, because every bank that pulls that bureau is held back. That keeps your pulls SPREAD across all three bureaus.

The bank research, application order, communication and follow-up are our job. That's the work our funding fee pays for. The yes or no belongs to each bank. That's why every bank on your list is one your file makes sense for.

5. FS-05 / The next stage — Every funding sequence you finish sets the start date for the next stage of your growth.

Round one goes out with the pulls spread across all three bureaus. Before the next round leaves, every NEW inquiry from round one gets disputed.

Before the next round, we review the new inquiries and dispute those that qualify. A funding sequence runs about six rounds.

Once a sequence wraps up, our system marks the day your file can start the next one. The Fundhub team gets that day too, so nobody has to remember it.

Your business grows in stages that way. Each funding sequence funds the stage you're in, and the next one is already dated before you need it.

Your growth runs on a plan, one funding sequence after another, all off the same personal file.

6. FS-06 / Four companies — My one personal file got $100,000 lines approved for four separate companies.

I run my own funding exactly this way. My one personal file got $100,000 lines approved for FOUR separate companies.

The banks approved every one of those lines on stated income, without a single income document.

All four came off the same personal file, and that's why our system works your personal file before it touches any company.

Each business gets reviewed on its own, starting with your personal file. The bank still decides whether to offer credit and on what terms.

Personal first, then each company on its own: that's the ORDER our system runs on your file too.

7. FS-07 / Your lender list — Your own lender list goes up on the call, with your card and your first bank filled in.

The five rules from the start print on your own lender list too, and on the call they print with YOUR card and YOUR first bank in them.

We run the soft pull right there on the call. It doesn't touch your score, and no bank sees it, because nothing has been applied for yet.

Rule one shows which of your cards to pay down first, and the balance it needs to reach before any application leaves.

Rule two names the bank on your list with the lowest score floor, so you know exactly which bank gets your file FIRST.

Your advisor shares the screen and walks your file with you. You see how much you qualify for based on where you're at right now, and what's holding back the rest.

From there you pick the path that fits: fund now, fix first, or both, and your lender list shows which step comes first.
