# B-roll shot lists — 2026-10-02 ads

Six shot lists, one per final script in `marketing/ads/scripts/2026-10-02.md` (commit e467a5fc). Only the spoken paragraphs under each `## Ad N` heading count. The notes under it are not spoken.

- **The clips:** 22 MP4s in `marketing/broll/out/ad-21/` to `marketing/broll/out/ad-26/`. Together they are 10.3 MB (after the 3D upgrade and the two BankPockets swaps). Each is 1080x1920 at 30 fps, H.264 (yuv420p, bt709), 2.5 to 3.5 seconds. `marketing/broll/out/` is in `.gitignore`, so the MP4s stay out of git.
- **Submagic:** upload each MP4 under My B-rolls and drop it at its start time. A clip opens on the words it shows, so line it up with the start of its line.
- **Start time:** words spoken before the clip ÷ 2.5 = seconds (150 words a minute). Words are counted with the ad checker's rule `[A-Za-z0-9'’$%-]+` (`scripts/ads/check-script.mjs`). Shown as m:ss, rounded down, with the exact seconds after it. The word totals match the scripts file for all six ads.
- **What is on screen:** every word and number comes from the line the clip sits on. The few exceptions come from the same sentence, the /roadmap or /watch page for the same idea, or the earlier sentence in the same ad that the line pays off. Each one is named under its table. No sample-client amounts, no scores, and no lender names: the lender rows stay blank.
- **Lines with no clip** stay on Chris: most hooks, the open-loop lines, the price line and the call to action.
- **Render one clip** (inside `marketing/broll/`): save its props block below as `props.json`, then run
  `npx remotion render src/index.ts <Template> out/ad-NN/<file>.mp4 --codec=h264 --props=props.json`.
  BankPockets clips add `--gl=angle`: without it this Mac's default renderer cuts slices out of the bank's 3D pockets.
  The kit's config sets the bt709 color tags. Every command is typed at the terminal; no render script is saved. That keeps the license on the $25 a month "Remotion for Creators" option.
