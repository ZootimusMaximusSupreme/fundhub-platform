SECTION: FAQ

Question:
Does the Funding Map replace my mortgage?

Answer:
The Funding Map begins with unsecured and contractor financing routes. Property-backed choices appear as a separate route only when you want to compare them.

Question:
Will the money go to me?

Answer:
That depends on the selected program. Some products fund the homeowner directly. Other programs pay an approved contractor, sometimes in project stages. The map shows that difference before the handoff.

Question:
Can two household incomes be used?

Answer:
Several researched providers support a co-applicant, co-borrower, cosigner, or joint applicant. The exact relationship and ownership rules depend on the selected provider.

Question:
What if I am self-employed?

Answer:
The intake has a self-employed path. Expect to organize personal and business tax returns, bank statements, ownership records, and a current profit-and-loss statement when requested.

Question:
What if the first offer is below the project amount?

Answer:
Fundhub keeps a second route ready. That may be another suitable provider category, a contractor program, a phased project plan, or a readiness path.

Question:
How much does the Funding Map cost?

Answer:
The initial Funding Map starts at $0.

Question:
How fast do I receive the route?

Answer:
After the required intake is complete, Fundhub delivers the route summary, document checklist, and next-step recommendation within two business days.
