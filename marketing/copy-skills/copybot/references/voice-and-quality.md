# Voice and quality — Fundhub version

The shared pack names a "voice and quality" file but did not include it. Chris, 2026-10-31: it held the original author's voice, so Fundhub writes its own. This page is Fundhub's: it says whose voice to use and what every piece must pass before Chris sees it. It does not add new Copybot technique; that stays in the other references.

## Voice

1. Read `references/owner-voice.md` and the sources it lists, in order, before drafting Chris's copy.
2. Write the way he talks to one person: certain, direct, plain. Let his phrases in, never as a costume of repeated slang.
3. The voice pass comes last and never changes a fact, a promise, a condition or the logic (`references/workflows.md`, step 6).

## Facts come from the offer, never from the copy

- Read the offer first (`.ai/rules/ad-know-the-offer.md`). Every claim, number, guarantee, price and step comes from an offer file, a live page, a green-lit script, a real approval, or Chris's own words in a saved spec.
- Sample numbers are labeled as samples and come from one sample file (`.ai/rules/sample-clients-consistent.md`).
- Proof comes from the real source picture (`.ai/rules/proof-cards-from-source.md`).
- A claim with no source is narrowed, cut, or flagged. It is never written as fact.

## Fundhub checks before Chris sees it

Every ad, VSL, email, text, push line, slideshow and page passes all of these:

- **Reading level:** ads and customer copy at 5th grade or lower.
- **Certainty:** no we think, we believe, you might, maybe, could, probably, likely, hopefully, chances are (`.ai/rules/copy-certainty.md`).
- **Day counts:** allowed when they match the written guarantee. The guarantee is "applications go out within 72 business hours"; approvals are never promised in 72 hours.
- **Banned words:** "number" in sales copy, "funding gap", "Funding Snapshot", "the biggest yes" (`.ai/rules/outcome-first-copy.md`); "round two" (`.ai/rules/funding-sequence-term.md`); "CRS" in customer copy (Chris, 2026-10-31).
- **Name:** Fundhub, exactly.
- **No SSN** before checkout step 3 (`.ai/rules/no-ssn-before-checkout-step-3.md`).
- **No recycled lines** across ads unless the numbers proved the line (`.ai/rules/ad-no-recycled-lines.md`).
- **Second line** does one job (`.ai/rules/ad-second-line.md`).
- **Ad scripts** also run the `fundhub-ad-writer` checker.
- **Copybot's own checks:** one central idea; every big claim has its support next to it; the opening's promise is paid off; nothing is invented; no CTA or offer added where the piece does not need one.

## Saying how good it is

Writing judgment is not a result. Say a draft is clearer or better supported; never say it will convert better until a test shows it (`references/workflows.md`, Analysis).
