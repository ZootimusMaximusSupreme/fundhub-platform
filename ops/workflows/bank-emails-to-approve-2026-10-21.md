# Bank emails — read these, then say "approved bank emails"

Nothing is sent to any bank until you approve. These are the exact words.

**How it sends**
- From: Fundhub Bank Partnerships <relationships@fundhub.ai>
- At the bottom of every email: the mailing address **218 Bostick Rd 64, Bowling Green, FL 33834**, plus "reply STOP" and an unsubscribe link. (The address comes from your signed agreements. Tell me if the footer should show a different one.)
- No more than 40 emails a day. Email 1, then 2, then 3. A banker who replies or says STOP gets no more.
- Words in {{double braces}} fill in by themselves: the banker's first name, the bank's name, the state's name.
