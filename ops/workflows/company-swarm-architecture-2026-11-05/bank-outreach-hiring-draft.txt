POST TITLE
Bank Partnerships Outbound Rep | Remote Egypt | US Hours

POST COPY
Fundhub is hiring a remote bank-outreach representative based in Egypt.

You will speak with US bankers, build professional relationships, and collect accurate information about business credit cards and lending products. Your work helps our team maintain a useful, current lender database.

What you will do:
- Contact assigned banks during their US business hours.
- Ask clear questions about product terms, eligibility, geographic availability and application steps.
- Record the bank, product, source, date and supporting evidence for each answer.
- Keep confirmed facts separate from unanswered questions or informal estimates.
- Maintain follow-up tasks and complete handoffs to the Fundhub team.

You should have clear spoken and written English, experience with B2B outbound calls, careful note-taking, reliable internet and a quiet workspace. Experience speaking with US businesses is useful. Bank or lending-product experience is a plus.

To apply, tell us about your B2B calling experience, your availability during US bank hours, and how you keep notes accurate after a call.

INITIAL SCREENING QUESTIONS
1. Are you based in Egypt, and what hours can you work in US Eastern Time? State your local time as well.
2. Describe one B2B outbound role: who did you call, what did a successful conversation produce, and how did you document it?
3. A banker gives you a product detail you cannot find online. How would you record it, verify it, and follow up?

SHORT INTERVIEW EXERCISE
Use a fictional bank product. The interviewer is the banker and the applicant is Fundhub's representative. Give the applicant this opening: "Our business card has a 0% introductory period, but approval depends on the applicant."
Ask the applicant to run a two-minute discovery call and write a short handoff afterward.

Look for:
- Clear, natural spoken English and a professional opening.
- Useful follow-up questions about the introductory period, later APR, fees, eligibility, geography and application route.
- Accurate notes that distinguish confirmed answers from unknowns.
- A named source/date and a sensible next follow-up.
Reject invented answers or presenting an informal banker statement as a guaranteed approval rule.
