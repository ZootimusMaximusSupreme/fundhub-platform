Objective
Build the first complete marketing package for Fundhub's real estate lending entry point as one ecosystem with two connected markets:
1. Homeowners seeking substantial home improvement financing.
2. Qualified borrowers and capital partners with strong, verifiable credit or income who want to participate in real estate opportunities.

The ecosystem must also preserve future products for both audiences. It is not limited to one loan or one offer.
