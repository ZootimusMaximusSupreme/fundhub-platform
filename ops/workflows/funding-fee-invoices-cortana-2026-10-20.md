## Workflow A — CRM fee truth

**Source rule and timing.** The owner-set rule in `docs/finance/CLOSEOUT-FEE-BASIS.md` is the sale's `agreed_success_fee_percent` (percent units; 10 means 10%) times the sum of that **one round's** `applications.approved_amount` where status is `Approved`, amount is positive, and `approval_excluded_at IS NULL`. `src/funding/success-fee.mjs` resolves this from `funding_round_sales → sales` and returns a named refusal for no confirmed approvals or no agreed rate. `funding_rounds.funded_amount` and its `approved_amount` summary are not invoice bases. The deposit is separate; current `src/workflows/f-07-funding-locked.mjs` bills the full fee with no deposit offset, consistent with `src/calculators/deal-math.mjs`. Marketing wording in `docs/finance/slo-offer-model-2026-09-18.md` says “deposit against” the fee, but this work preserves the shipped math rather than changing that policy.
