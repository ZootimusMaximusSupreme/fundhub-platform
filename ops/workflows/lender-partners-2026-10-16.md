## How to sign up

Neither bank has a self-serve partner signup page.

**Newtek** — email a description of Fundhub to **klowy@newtekone.com** (the referral partner address on newtekone.com/partnership). Newtek says it works with ISOs and business brokers and tracks referrals in its NewTracker system.

**Live Oak** — referral partners sign Live Oak's standard referral agreement with its lending team; the fee is paid after the loan closes and funds. Routes in:
- Email **andrew.kennedy@liveoak.bank** (listed as Live Oak's contact on a preferred-partner directory; no dedicated partnerships inbox exists)
- Call **866-438-9393** or the HQ line **866-518-0286** and ask for the SBA referral partner team
- Contact form at liveoakbank.com/sba-loan-experts
- Message a Live Oak SBA lender on LinkedIn

Line to use: "I run Fundhub, a business funding company, and I have SBA-ready clients I want to refer. Can you connect me with someone on your SBA lending team about a referral partner agreement?"

Have ready: Fundhub LLC name, address, website, rough monthly count of SBA-ready clients.

## What's public so far

**Live Oak (SBA)**
- #1 SBA 7(a) lender by dollar volume, FY2025: 2,280 approvals, $2.8B+
- Live Oak Express for SBA loans under $350,000 — approvals in days
- 680 minimum FICO on its own site (older reviews say 650)
- 2+ years in business; some products want 3 years of tax returns
- 30–45 days to approval and closing on standard SBA
- Up to 90% financed; 10% equity injection on acquisitions
- Under $500,000, SBA collateral and equity rules relax to bank policy
- Personal guarantee from every owner at 20%+
- No hard personal inquiry to start

**Newtek (SBA)**
- SBA Preferred Lender, Boca Raton, FL
- Prefers 650 credit, flexible; 700 for startups
- Generally 2–3 years in business
- Pulls personal and business credit reports
- Common complaints: heavy paperwork, long processing

**Accord Business Funding (MCA)**
- Partner-only direct funder ("funds a submission or it dies with us")
- Soft pull only
- No published minimum credit, revenue, or time in business
- Reviews: quick underwriting decisions, competitive ISO bonuses, offers sometimes slower

**SBA in general**
- SBA Preferred Lenders make the credit decision in-house; the SBA guarantees 75–85% of the loan
- Smaller 7(a) loans require a business credit screen (FICO SBSS)
- Existing MCAs count as debt against cash flow
- Any referral or agent fee on an SBA loan is disclosed on SBA Form 159

## Questions for every lender call

1. Which industries do you fund, and which do you refuse?
2. What are the top reasons a file gets declined?
3. Minimum personal credit score, and which bureau do you pull?
4. Do you pull business credit, and from which bureau?
5. Minimum time in business and monthly revenue?
6. Rates, terms, and fees?
7. Do you file a UCC lien, and is it a blanket lien?
8. Will you fund a client who already has other loans or MCAs, and in what position?
9. How long from submission to funding?
10. What documents do you want with every submission?
11. What do you pay partners, and when?

Merge with the lender sheet questions in the CRM once compared.
