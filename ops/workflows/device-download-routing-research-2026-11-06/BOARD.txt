STRICT DEFAULT FOR CHRIS'S CERTAINTY REQUIREMENT
Browser OS signals are suggestions, not proof of physical hardware. UA and
UA Client Hints can both be overridden; embedded WKWebView can set its own UA.
Therefore do not promise 100% automatic device identification.
Default public entry to Open in browser and a neutral Choose my device action.
No automatic Apple badges/download links. Reveal iPhone/Mac download choices
only after the visitor deliberately selects that target device. This also
supports Windows users who actually want to add their separate iPhone.
Detection may preselect/suggest a device inside that chooser, never force a
redirect or reveal an Apple download before the deliberate selection.
Android/Windows/Linux/ChromeOS, unknown and conflicting signals stay browser
by default. Never infer Apple from Safari/AppleWebKit, screen width or !mobile.
iPad may send a Mac-identical UA. Mac+touch is an ambiguity guard, not proof
of iPad. Missing Client Hints is normal on browsers without that API.
Even a self-declared Mac must not get a native download until the separate
public Mac release URL and supported OS/architecture are verified.
Native apps know their own build platform, but a Mac cannot infer whether the
user's other phone is iPhone or Android, or which computer an iPhone owner has.
