1. PRE-CALL CONTENT LIBRARY
Create roughly 66 distinct short clips from the 22 core lessons (about three cuts per lesson). Deliver the main objection answer or insight in the first 15 seconds. Content ads can play natively without a hard CTA. Unbooked viewers get a separate direct-response booking ad through the existing VSL, survey, and calendar. Booked viewers never get a second book/confirm prompt.
Chris's stretch target is an average of 66 distinct clips watched for at least 15 seconds before the sales call. Measure actual unique clips watched per booked person, the time available before the call, show rate, and close rate. Jeremy's 15-20 exposures in 72 hours is the first delivery benchmark; increase only if observed results justify it. In Chris's Drive example, a 15-second ThruPlay cost $0.89, so 66 such views would cost about $58.74 per booked person before uneven delivery. A $10/day test cannot be presented as delivering 66 to everyone. 2. UNPAID-CLIENT RECOVERY EXPERIMENT — BUILD AFTER EXISTING QUEUE
Simulated case: A buyer accepts a Blueprint, repair, or funding offer on the call and receives the correct checkout link. Two hours later, the saved payment link is still unpaid and no verified payment receipt exists. This is an agreed offer awaiting initial payment. Keep it distinct from an issued success-fee invoice; existing AR-01/02/03/04 already chases that invoice path. Do not use sale.closed as an unpaid trigger because it is a paid-sale signal.

Split test: Control gets the current follow-up. Test gets a short content sequence. Clip 1 explains the immediate next step; clip 2 answers what the fee starts; clip 3 addresses a failed checkout; clip 4 invites a direct reply about timing. Each clip gives the point in the first 15 seconds. Email/text can carry the exact pay or help link. Any Meta clip is generic and never shows an individual's balance or payment status. Do not send both this sequence and a conflicting A/R chase.

Build checks: map payment_links ask, processor receipt, and current follow-up states; draft the four clips; simulate both groups with fake clients; stop immediately on verified payment, void/replacement, refund/dispute, agreed plan, opt-out, or staff pause. Use the current Finance OS ledger and send gate. Measure seven-day verified payment rate, recovered cash less ad cost, time to pay, support replies, opt-outs, and duplicate or post-payment chases. No live test starts from this queue entry.

Research lead: A Local Government Association randomized trial found simpler, personalized, action-focused reminders improved repayment in a different setting.
