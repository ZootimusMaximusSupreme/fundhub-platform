### Verdict definitions and standard protocol

**PASS:** exact trace/test run, parameters, role, tenant, read-after-refresh, event/task/document IDs (redacted in public board), and production or isolated test label recorded. **FAIL:** reproducible code or test failure with expected/actual and linked fix. **NOT LIVE PROVED (NLP):** candidate code, test names, read-only page inventory, or CI-only green without complete role/provider evidence. **N/A:** not a verified live offering or action, with proof. No inferred PASS. Each interactive control must also prove keyboard, phone view, loading/empty/network-error, auth and cross-org refusal, persistence, and replay. Operational controls that intentionally send messages/mail/credit are exercised only with stubs.
