# Outcome-first copy

**Owner law (2026-09-29):** Chris, on the `/roadmap` page: "We're not speaking in outcomes, we're just giving them facts."

## Always

- Open every section, card and sample popup with why the buyer cares, in one bold sentence. Then give the facts. Short beats complete.
- When he asks to fix or reorganize a page, stay on the page copy and order. Ask before a side job that renders assets or touches files outside the page.
- Say it plainly: "get approved for the most", "maximum funding", "left on the table", "How Much You Qualify For".

## Never (owner-banned 2026-09-29)

- The word "number" in sales copy ("Your Funding Number", "highest number").
- "The biggest yes" and lines like it.
- "Funding gap" and "Funding Snapshot".
- Emojis on the page, unless he asks.

"Unlimited funding" stays. That is the owner's call.
