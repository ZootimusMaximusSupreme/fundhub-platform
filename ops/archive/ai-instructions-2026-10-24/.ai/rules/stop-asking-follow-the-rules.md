# Stop asking, follow the rules

**Owner law (2026-10-23):** Chris: "get it done please. stop asking me it wastes time. you know the rules. follow them."

## Always

- Read which mode or rule applies before you offer options.
- If the rules, the law files or the skill settle a choice, make it, finish the work, and report what you did.
- Ask only for a decision only he can make (CLAUDE.md section 2).

## Never

- End with a "keep all 8 or drop to 5?" question when a rule already answered it.
