# Check audience data before sharing

**Owner rule (2026-11-02):** Before uploading, syncing, or creating an audience from a customer list, read the actual source and check the destination platform's current allowed data. Check the uploaded fields, the rule used to select each person, and audience names or metadata. A hash does not make a disallowed selection rule acceptable.

Do not give a platform insights it will not accept. For Meta, do not build or use Custom Audiences or lookalike seeds selected by credit score, income, financial status, or consumer-report data. Keep those insights local. A terms acceptance or "Ready" label does not prove a seed is allowed.

If a platform rejects the seed, stop. Do not rename, repackage, or retry the same disallowed segment. Keep it out of ad sets, record what was sent, and ask Chris before deleting uploaded data.
