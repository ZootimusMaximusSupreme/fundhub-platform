# No recycled lines across ads

**Owner law (2026-10-15):** Chris: "You can't just copy and paste lines from other ads. That's the rule. You only do that when that's a high converting phrase. The point is to test it."

## Always

- Write every line of a new ad fresh. Each ad is a test, and a test needs its own words.
- Before handing over a script, compare it to the other ads in the batch and to the filmed ads. Rewrite any sentence that matches one of them.
- Reuse a line only when the numbers show it is a high-converting phrase, and say which ad proved it.
- Owner-set terms still repeat as single words or short phrases: Fundhub, "maximum amount of funding", "credit optimization". Whole sentences do not.

## Never

- Copy a sentence, a call to action, or a proof story from another ad because it already worked in a draft.
- Reuse the same opener shape across a batch (for example "This is for you if" in two ads).
- No "Not another ___, but..." openers. "Not another ___, but…". (owner-set 2026-10-02)

## Example

```text
Ad 13 says: "This is for you if you've been paying for growth out of the same account that pays the mortgage."

❌ Ad 16 opens its qualify line with "This is for you if ..."
✅ Ad 16 says it a new way, unless the ad report shows Ad 13's line is the reason it converts.
```
