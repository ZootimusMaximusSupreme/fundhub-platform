# Speak with 100% certainty in scripts

**Owner law (2026-10-15):** Chris: "We want 100% certainty throughout these scripts, always. That's a rule. Never use we think, you might, any of that type of shit. It needs to be 110% certain."

## Always

- Write every ad, VSL, thank-you video and green screen note in certain words. State it as fact.
- Say "how much you qualify for based on where you're at right now". This replaces the older "how much we think you'll qualify for".

## Never

- we think, we believe, we feel
- you might, you may, maybe, could, could be, can possibly
- probably, likely, most likely, hopefully, chances are
- "Maybe your bank denied you" style openers. Use "When a bank denies you..." instead.
- Never state a day count. The page's refund line says "within 7 days". Ads never state a day count. (owner-set 2026-10-02)

## Example

```text
❌ "Your advisor will show you how much we think you'll qualify for."
✅ "Your advisor will show you how much you qualify for based on where you're at right now."
```
