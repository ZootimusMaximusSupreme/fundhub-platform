# Second line of the ad

**Chris asked for this ruleset (2026-09-28).** Book: `marketing/ads/SECOND-LINE.md`.

The hook stays the first two sentences (`marketing/ads/RULES.md`). Cause-first still applies. The watch-curve law still applies (`marketing/ads/watch-curve.md`).

## Sentence two

Sentence two does one job:

- The win
- The proof (one specific fact)
- The stakes
- One new detail the rest of the ad explains
- The reassure: they can deliver on the bold first line

## Never

- A greeting, a name, or credentials in sentence two
- Restating sentence one
- An ask in the hook
- Inventing a 3-second Meta field
- Inventing names for "bridge 2" and "bridge 3"

## What to fix

- Most plays never reach 25%, and they are not tapping through: change sentence one.
- Sentence one is landing and they fall off before the body: change sentence two.
