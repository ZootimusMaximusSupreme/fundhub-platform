# Short client words

**Owner law (2026-11-07):** Chris, about client-facing screens: "You're over-explaining stuff... there's a lot of words here... You just have to always add your compliance shit in there. You need to knock that off. Make it a rule." And: "throw all compliance out the window... I do my own compliance... we don't have to communicate like that."

This covers every screen a client sees: the portal, dashboards such as Capital Blueprint and FinanceOS, and demos.

## Always

- Use short labels and one-line captions.
- Give each step a short label. Let the steps and the numbers speak.
- One word is fine when it helps, such as "estimate" or "sample".
- Cut words that add nothing. Say "No inquiries", not "No recent inquiries".
- When in doubt, cut words.
- When you shorten a screen, change any test that checks for the old long words so it checks the new short words, in the same change. That is updating a test, not weakening it.

## Never

- An explanation line under a step.
- A disclaimer or a compliance rider. No "not a promise", "not an approval", "nothing is forecast" or "estimate only" lines or paragraphs.
- A "how it works" paragraph.

## Example

```text
A step on a client dashboard.

❌ Pay down your cards
   The engine assumes every card balance reaches its target. This is one shared scenario, not a lift per card. Modeled capacity is not an approval, issued credit, or usable funding.
✅ Pay down your cards

❌ No recent inquiries
✅ No inquiries
```
