# Animations land on the spoken words

**Owner law (2026-10-23):** Chris: "Get the transcript to find those anchor words, then readjust." Animations go on a finished video last, and each one appears when the words it shows are spoken. Skill: `.cursor/skills/fundhub-video-animation-overlay/SKILL.md`.

## Always

- Find the real word times first (transcript, or the burned-in captions). Never use the plan's even-pace starts as if they were real.
- Start each clip so its payoff lands on its words. Stretch a clip inside its catalog length range when the speech runs longer.
- See-through clips run in overlay mode: no overlaps, and nothing else. The 4 s gap, 3 s cap and 35% share are full-frame rules.
- Cut him out and put the animations behind him (`--behind`); vary side, height and tuck from clip to clip. Words clear of the face box and the captions; only decoration goes behind him.
- Use the script's own words. Label sample-client numbers as a sample. Never invent a number, bank, name or claim.
- Test copy first. The original video is never changed. Save every round to the repo.

## Never

- Drop animations for a limit without checking which mode it belongs to.
- Ask Chris to approve a choice this rule settles.

He says "you pay $32, and there's no impact on your score" at 12.9 to 15.4 s.

❌ Start the clip at the anchor's first word (10.8 s) and show "No impact" at 12.5 s.
✅ The strip shows "No impact" 1.7 s into the clip, so start at 12.7 s.
