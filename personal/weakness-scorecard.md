## How a score moves

- A score goes up only on proof: a number, a finished thing, or a logged rep.
- Each re-score: write the new number, the proof, and the date in the log at the bottom.

Scale:

- 1–3: it holds the business back every week.
- 4–5: it works some of the time.
- 6–7: it works most weeks without a push.
- 8–10: it is a strength.

## Guardrails for all nine

1. **One build open at a time.** Marketing work runs next to it. A new idea goes on the Later list at the bottom and waits.
2. **One course at a time.** It has to serve an active weakness, and it gets used within 48 hours.
3. **Done means walked.** A real or test client goes through every step, and Chris sees the result himself, before anything is called done.
4. **Smallest version first.** Before a build, write one line: "A real client can use ___ by ___." The date is within 7 days. Anything bigger goes on Later.
5. **Write before asking.** For copy and marketing calls, Chris writes his own one-line answer before asking Claude.

- **Guardrail:** one build open. New ideas go on the Later list.
- **Daily rep:** at the end of each day, name the one open build and its next step.
- **Transurfing tool:** pendulums. A shiny idea or a competitor's page pulls attention. Write it on Later and go back to the open build.
- **Moves the score:** 2 builds in a row finished before the next one started → 5. 4 in a row → 6.

### 2. Over-engineering — 4 → 6
