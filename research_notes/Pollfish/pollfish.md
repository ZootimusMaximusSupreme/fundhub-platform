# Pollfish — what it is and how Fundhub can use it

Saved 2026-10-13. Link Chris sent: https://www.pollfish.com/

## What it is

Pollfish is a do-it-yourself survey tool. You write a survey, pick who should answer it, and real people answer it fast. It is owned by Prodege. The people who answer are Prodege members: they earn rewards for shopping, playing games, and taking surveys.

- Panel: 250M+ people worldwide.
- Targeting: 7,000+ traits. Age, state, zip, household income, education, purchase history, and "financial product usage." It also has B2B (business professional) targeting, custom screening questions, and quotas.
- Credit score is not a built-in trait. To get "700+ credit" or "owns a business" you ask screening questions. Those answers are self-reported.
- Free account. You pay only per answer.
- Has an API (survey creation and data collection), but the docs and access are not public. You get it through their sales team.

## Price

Base is $0.95 per answer. Add-ons stack on top of that, per answer:

| Add-on | Extra per answer |
|---|---|
| 1 screening question | +$0.50 |
| 2 screening questions | +$1.00 |
| 3 screening questions | +$1.75 |
| 4 screening questions | +$2.75 |
| Each advanced demographic (high income, etc.) | +$0.55 |
| Each quota | +$0.55 |
| Price sensitivity method | +$0.50 |

Example: 300 answers, about 10 questions, 2 screening questions (owns a business, credit 700+), high income. That is $0.95 + $1.00 + $0.55 = $2.50 each, so about $750. Pollfish says the final price can go up for narrow targeting, so expect business-owner targeting to cost more and take longer.

Full-service projects (they run it for you) start at $5,000. Enterprise starts at $10,000 a year.

## How it helps Fundhub

### 1. Real stats for ad hooks (best use)

Chris's hook rule: the hook has to tell the viewer something insightful they don't know. A survey can create that fact.

Survey 300 business owners with 700+ credit. Ask what they believe about funding. The answers become hook lines Fundhub owns, for example "we asked 300 business owners with 700+ credit how many banks they applied to" or what share of them have never seen their file the way a lender reviews it. Nobody else in the industry has these numbers, and every number is real. One study can feed many ads.

### 2. Cut weak hooks before filming

Show 5 to 10 written hooks to a screened group. Ask which one makes them want to keep watching. Drop the bottom ones before filming.

Limit: these people are paid to answer and most are not shopping for funding. Meta results stay the real score. Use this only to cut the clear losers, never to pick a winner over live ad data.

### 3. Price testing

The price sensitivity add-on (+$0.50) asks people at what price an offer feels too cheap, fair, and too expensive. Useful for setting price on a new offer, like the FH Consulting range ($1,000 to $20,000) or Capital Blueprint.

## Honest take

Fundhub's buyers already know credit card stacking and want capital now. Pollfish panel members are regular people earning rewards. Some will match on paper (business owner, good credit), but they are not the ones searching for funding today. So Pollfish is weak for predicting who buys. It is strong for creating true, surprising stats about business owners and credit, which is exactly what a good hook needs.

## First test to run

One study, about 300 answers, about $750:

1. Screen: owns a business, credit 700+ (self-reported).
2. Ask 6 to 8 questions about what they believe banks look at, how many cards they think they could get, and whether they have ever applied for business credit.
3. Pull the 3 most surprising numbers. Write hooks from them.
