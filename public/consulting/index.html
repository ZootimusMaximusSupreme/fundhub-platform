FAQ

Short answers.

Do you run the agency for us?

No. We consult, set up the tools, and work beside your team for the term you buy. Your people still own the clients and the accounts.

Which tools do you use?

It depends on the agency. The work covers AI tools, offer tools, marketing management systems, and other tools only when they help. We name them in the plan. We do not lock you into a pile of software.

Do you guarantee results?

No. We do not promise revenue, lead counts, or any other result. You get the consulting, the plan, and the setup for the engagement you buy.

When do I pay?

Not on this page. After the consult, the price is on the proposal and at checkout. A payment plan, if one appears, is the payment company’s plan.

What is the refund policy?

Full refund within 14 days of purchase if we have not sent the written plan and have not held a working session. Details are in the Refund Policy.
