You are joining SmartCredit®, a credit monitoring product. This is a separate membership with a separate company. You pay SmartCredit® directly, every month. It is not part of anything you buy from Fundhub Credit Solutions LLC, and buying one does not get you the other.

What it costs

This is a monthly membership. Build costs $29.99 every month. Protect costs $19.99 every month. There is no free trial. You will be charged every month until you cancel.
