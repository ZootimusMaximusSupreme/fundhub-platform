WHICH ANGLE AND WHICH HOOK ARE WORKING
LabelAdsSpendPeople arrived People bookedCost per booked person Hook rateHold rate
A dash means we do not know. A 0 means we counted and it really was zero. They are never the same thing. Hover a dash and it says which reason it was. Blank spend means no day was reported for those ads. A blank hook rate means there was no video to measure — a photo ad has no hook rate and never will have one.
Hook rate is how many people kept watching past the opening, out of everyone the ad was put in front of. Hold rate is how many of those got three quarters of the way through. Meta counts "past the opening" at two seconds of watching. It does not publish a three-second count at all, so this is close to but not the same number Meta's own Ads Manager shows next to the words "hook rate". Cost per booked person is spend divided by the people who booked — on a sample too small to divide it is not worked out at all, and the reason is printed in the cell instead of a made-up number. Ads counts every ad that label has ever had; the money covers only the window above, which is why each row also says how many of those ads reported inside it.
