Touch

Tap: pause, and tap again to roll on. Double tap: scroll mode, so you can drag or flick the words. Tap to roll from there. Hold a line to change its words; it saves by itself.

Keyboard: Space, Enter or Page Down plays and pauses. The arrows change the speed. Page Up restarts the take. At the end of a script, Space is Got it and Page Up is Another take.
