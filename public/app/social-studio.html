How the copy check works

Your words are read against the rules this business has to follow. A post that breaks one is refused and kept, with the reason written on it.

What you see here is a rehearsal, not the final answer. The rules used for this preview are a copy held inside this page. If someone changes a rule, this preview does not change with it. The answer that counts is the one you get when you save the post.

These are ordinary posts on your own accounts, not paid adverts, so the rules that only apply to paid adverts are skipped, and nothing about who sees the post is checked. The credit-repair rules, the claims rules and the disclosure rules apply in full — your words are judged exactly as an advert would be.

If the check itself ever breaks, it refuses the post rather than letting it through.

If a picture is ever attached, the check does not look at it. It only reads the words.

DraftWritten, but not sent anywhere yet. You can still change your mind. WaitingChecked and lined up. It will go out at its set time. Sending nowGoing out right now. This lasts a second or two. A post that sits here means something crashed. SentIt went out. It is live on the account. Could not be sentThe system tried three times and could not get it out. Needs a rewriteThe copy check refused it. It will never go out. Write a new one.
A post goes out at the time set on it. A post with no send time never goes out on its own, and a post on an account that cannot publish waits rather than fails.
Emptying the time box and pressing "Queue it" does not take away a time that is already saved — the post keeps that time and still goes out. To stop a post going out, throw it away.
A refused post is kept, with its reasons on it. It can never be sent. There is no unblock, no override and no way to ask for an exception. To fix the wording, write a new post.
There are no like, view, click or share figures anywhere in the system, so none are shown here. Nothing records them.
A line is written before every attempt to send, so a post that crashes half way still leaves a record. "Undo recorded" does not mean a post can be taken back. It means an undo note was written down, and nothing in the system can act on it.
Best times to post
All times here are UTC. An account can also have a time zone saved on it, but nothing uses it — showing these times in it would show a time that is not the time a post goes out. The next time is found by looking 8 days ahead. If no times are set, no time is picked and none is made up.
