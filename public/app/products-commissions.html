COMMISSION RULES
WHY RATES ARE NEVER OVERWRITTEN
A commission rate is a dated fact, not a setting. Changing one closes the current version when the new rate starts and opens a new version. Nothing is destroyed. A payout that already accrued keeps pointing at the version that was open the day it was earned, so re-running last quarter's ledger produces last quarter's numbers.
