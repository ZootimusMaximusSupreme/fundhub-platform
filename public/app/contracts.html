How this works

Build a contract once. Use it many times. Upload a PDF, or type the words here. Add the blanks people fill in. Say who has to sign.

Staff pick it later. When they send a contract from a client’s screen, this one shows up in their list. They never have to write it again.

To see contracts you already sent, and who has signed them, go to Documents.
