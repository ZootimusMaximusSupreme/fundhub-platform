How Fundhub Works

01 · Who we are

A funding company with its own underwriting

Fundhub has close to a decade in funding, hundreds of files and thousands of data points behind it. Our own software, UnderwriteIQ, reviews every file the way a lender does.

02 · What we sell

Credit card stacking and credit optimization

Business owners come to us to get funded with 0% intro APR business cards, and to optimize their credit when the file needs work first. Most of them already know what stacking is when they book.

03 · Where leads come from

Our ads and our affiliate partners

Paid ads and affiliate partners send the leads, so you work people who already asked to talk to us.

04 · Who does well here

Coachable people who track their numbers

You'll do well here if you take coaching, know your numbers every week, and show up to every huddle.
