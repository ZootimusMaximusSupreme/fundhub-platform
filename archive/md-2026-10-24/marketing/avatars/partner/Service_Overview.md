## 2. The Client Journey (The Process)

Told from the client's side, first touch to operating partner. No step in the ladder is a prerequisite for the next; the client can enter at any rung.

**Step 1 — The $27 Decline Autopsy (the first taste).**
You hand over a dead deal from your folder of one-word declines. Instead of "does not meet criteria," you get the actual cause — you watch FundHub read a file the way a fulfillment operation reads one. This is the moment you experience the difference between deal flow and fulfillment firsthand. Flat $27, nothing recurring.

**Step 2 — The $47/mo Winner's Board (ongoing proof).**
You see the funding vertical's ads sorted by survival time — evidence of what actually runs, not theory. Cancel any time.

**Step 3 — The $297 Live Trial (optional, never required).**
Seven days under **your own brand** — real calls, real files, the actual partner view. Not a sandbox, not a recorded walkthrough. You operate the front of the machine before you buy it. (The $297 does not credit toward the $10,000 — you're told this up front, on the page.)

**Step 4 — The Application and the Review Call (the human gate).**
A short application, a human read of it, then a call where **a person decides — never a lender, never a score.** Your own credit file is never a requirement and never a gate. If you're accepted and need financing, it exists down to a 405 FICO — as a payment method only. If you don't fit, the affiliate track (20% direct / 5% second tier, no fee, no floor) is offered as a genuine alternative, not a consolation prize.

**Step 5 — Agreement and Provisioning (your company appears).**
Application → Vetting → Agreement → Org Provisioned → Live. Your brand, your domain, your registered phone numbers, your payment processor, your branded client portal, your pipelines and dashboards, AI agents seeded from FundHub's prompts and tuned to your voice. Your ad account is connected — free, and required, because that is how attribution actually works.

**Step 6 — Live From Day One (you operate the front; they run the back).**
Your book opens. You generate and close deals. Behind you, FundHub runs everything else: soft pulls, underwriting, letter generation, inquiry removal, document collection, lender submission, chasing money. You watch every file through your partner view — your slice, your clients, where every file stands.

**Step 7 — The Standard (the floor).**
Ten funding clients a month, enforced. Below the floor, the partnership ends. This is stated on the page, not buried in a contract — the floor is what keeps the operation an operation.

## 7. The Hidden Mechanisms

The reasons The Infrastructure Transplant actually works — the proof logic beneath the name:

1. **It's live tissue, not a kit.** The back office isn't assembled for the partner at signup; it is the same operation FundHub runs for its own clients daily. There is no ramp-up period and no "will it work" risk, because it is already working the day the partner's org is provisioned. (VSL cut: most funding brokers have never funded a single business — FundHub *is* the fulfillment company others outsource to.)

2. **The lender list stays sealed — and that's why the split holds.** The relationships are never shared with anyone at any price. Protecting the moat is explicitly "part of why the 50% is worth what it's worth." The partner's margin is defended by the very secrecy that frustrates them in the FAQ.

3. **The human gate protects every partner's brand.** Acceptance by a person on a review call — never a lender, never a score — means the transplant only goes into hosts who can sustain it. Financing to 405 FICO removes the credit filter without removing the fit filter.

4. **The ten-client floor keeps the organ alive.** Fulfillment at scale only works with volume flowing through it. Enforcing the floor (partnership ends below it) is not a penalty; it is the mechanism that keeps the shared operation worth 50% to everyone attached to it.

5. **The split that never moves removes the owner's usual fear.** Not introductory, not tiered, not renegotiated by volume or tenure. The partner can scale aggressively because growth cannot be taxed later — which is exactly what makes partners scale, which feeds the floor, which feeds the operation.

6. **Attribution is wired in from day one.** The required (and free) ad-account connection means every dollar of partner marketing is traceable to files and revenue. "Nobody runs blind" — the partner sees cause and effect on their own book through the partner view.

7. **The ladder pre-proves the transplant before the $10,000 is ever asked for.** The $27 autopsy lets the prospect *watch the organ work* on their own dead file; the $297 trial lets them *wear it* under their own brand for seven days. By the review call, the mechanism isn't claimed — it has been experienced. (This is also why the $10,000 page never sees cold traffic.)

8. **Radical candor is load-bearing, not decorative.** The floor, the sealed lender list, the partner-view limit, the trial not crediting, marketing as an add-on — all disclosed on the page. "We'd rather you know that on this page than find out later." In a vertical full of $3K–$5K promise-sellers, disclosed limits are themselves proof the operation is real.
