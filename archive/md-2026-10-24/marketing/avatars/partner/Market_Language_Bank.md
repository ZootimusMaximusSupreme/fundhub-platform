## 7. The trade's dictionary (verbatim from the phrasebook vein)

THE TRADE'S DICTIONARY (MCA / business-funding broker world, mined from DailyFunder thread titles + Google-indexed snippets; direct page access was proxy-blocked, see unreachable). Term = meaning [emotional charge]: ISO = independent sales organization, what a broker shop calls itself; you "sign up with" funders, funders are "onboarding new ISO's" [identity word, worn with pride]. shop = a brokerage ("new iso shop", "a decent shop") [tribal]. file / files = a merchant's application in motion; deals become "files" the moment docs exist [possessive — a stolen file is a personal injury]. backdoored / backdooring = your file leaked around you and got funded without you; the trade's cardinal sin AND its default excuse for every lost deal [rage + paranoia; counter-charge exists: "why do people think every deal they lose is back doored" / deBanked's "Backdooring Deals? You're a Loser"]. backdoor shop = an outfit that exists to shop your files. my book / book of business = your merchants plus their renewals; the asset you fear a funder will steal [pride + theft-fear]. renewals = merchant re-ups ~halfway through; where the real money is; a shop is judged on whether it "pays renewals" [desire, and dread of being cut off]. residuals = renewal income in recruiting copy [the dream]. the split = your percentage at a shop (25-40% new deals is the quoted norm) [fairness/status scorekeeping]. points = commission units on the funded amount ("Over 12 points") [scorekeeping]. buy rate / sell rate / max upsell = funder's base factor rate vs. what you mark it up to; the spread is your pay [insider math]. PSF = professional service fee charged to the merchant on top ("hit em with the psf") [contested — half the forum calls it fair, half calls it sleaze]. stips = stipulations, the docs underwriting demands before wiring [friction, eye-roll]. subs / submissions = deals sent in (funders run "Subs@" inboxes). declines = files nobody funded — and a sellable inventory ("Stop wasting your declined files") [defeat recycled into product]. the box = a funder's credit criteria; files land "outside another funder's box"; good funders keep "your box tight" [shrug, fate]. paper = deal-quality caste: A paper, B paper, C/D [status ladder for merchants and funders alike]. position = which advance in line you are (1st/2nd position, "lend behind" OnDeck). stacking = piling advances on an already-advanced merchant [dirty word, daily practice; lawsuits attached]. too deep = merchant has too many positions. NSFs / negative days = bounced payments and negative-balance days on the bank statements — the first thing underwriting counts [triage vocabulary]. soft pull / hard pull = credit checks that don't/do leave a mark. funding call = funder's final verification call with the merchant — where deals die. killed in final = died at the last step ("Bitty killing everything in final") [gut-punch]. clawback = funder takes back paid commission when the merchant defaults early [fury; "CLAWBACKS happening?"]. direct funder / direct lender = has its own money, not another broker in disguise — everyone claims it, few are [suspicion]. super broker = a shop you broker through to reach funders you can't get signed with [half service, half insult — "just a super broker with a bunch of white labels"]. white label = funder's paper under your brand. syndication = putting your own money into your deals alongside the funder [skin-in-the-game status]. UCC leads / UCC lists / aged leads = lead lists scraped from public filings of already-funded merchants; cheap, hammered to death [contempt + dependence]. live transfers = merchant delivered live on the phone, "100% exclusive" [hope sold to the burned]. one off deals = funder takes your deal once, no relationship. reverse consolidation = the product pitched to over-stacked merchants. merchant = the client, always "merchant," never "customer." wild west / "wild wild west" = the trade's own name for itself [half boast, half complaint]. Register overall: betrayal-vigilance (BEWARE / STAY AWAY / scumbags threads), scorekeeping (points, splits, books), gold-rush status (7 figures, same-day funding), and exclamation-mark grievance — titles carry multiple !!!! and misspellings left standing.
