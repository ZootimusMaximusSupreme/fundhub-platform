# Copy directives — partner funnel (owner-set)

Standing rules for every piece of Ascension copy. The rewrite reads this
first, alongside Core_Avatar_Profile.md and Market_Language_Bank.md.

## 1. The hero's journey is the spine (owner-set 2026-08-31)

Brandon is the hero. FundHub is the guide. FundHub is never the hero.

The story the funnel tells, stage by stage:

| Journey stage | Brandon's version | Where it lives in the funnel |
|---|---|---|
| Ordinary world | Renting the relationships he finds; the 15-20% slice; the folder of dead deals | Cold ads, top of the $27 page |
| Call to adventure | "There's a version of this where your name is on the company" | $27 autopsy: watch the machine read HIS file |
| Refusal / fear | Backdoored once; paid for a course that wasn't a business; one hand over his wallet | Named openly on-page - the scam-fear IS the story beat |
| Meeting the guide | FundHub shows the machine instead of claiming things: printed limits, the sealed lender list, the floor | Autopsy result -> Winner's Board -> offer page candor sections |
| Crossing the threshold | Seven days wearing his own brand, real calls, real files | $297 Live Trial (optional - a threshold, never a toll) |
| The ordeal / decision | The review call. A person decides, not a score | Application -> review call |
| Transformation | Owner: his brand, his book, his renewals, 50/50 on funding and repair | Partnership, day one |
| Return with the elixir | He recruits the next Brandon ($2,000 bonus); or the affiliate path for those not ready | Downsell + referral loop |

Rules this imposes on the copy:
- Every page speaks in second person about HIS story. FundHub appears as
  the guide with a plan, never as the protagonist with a trophy shelf.
- The scam-scar is a story beat, not an objection to dodge: name the
  refusal stage in his words before he raises it.
- Each rung of the ladder is one threshold in the same single story -
  never five separate pitches.

## 2. Use their words (owner-set)

Copy is written from the Market Language Bank - the market's own recurring
phrases, mirrored back. No invented slang, no marketer-speak where a
documented phrase exists.

## 3. Guardrails carried from the avatar (hard rules)

- Never "we only get paid when you do" - false; the narrow true claim is
  the 50/50 backdoor-is-bad-math argument.
- Never "50% of everything" - the split is funding and repair only.
- No perpetuity language until a written contract term exists.
- No income claims, no credit-outcome claims, no lender named. Ever.
