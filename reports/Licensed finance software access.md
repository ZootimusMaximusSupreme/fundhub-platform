# Ghost Guard Can License Finance Tools

Ghost Guard should **start with CRS’s official sandbox and platform-partner application**, describe its actual financing-services business and state plainly that it does not originate loans. Public programs offer legitimate routes for software platforms, integrators and resellers; lending status alone does not establish eligibility or disqualify the company. Evaluate competitor-equivalent capabilities through the vendors’ own licensed products and test environments. The recommended sequence is CRS first, a Plaid synthetic-data sandbox alongside it, then a LoanPro trial if Ghost Guard needs a complete financing workflow. Before building the CRS integration, obtain the exact enabled business-report product and its account-specific ordering documentation: **CRS LexisNexis API access does not establish entitlement to CRS Top Business reports**. Production eligibility, report-sharing rights, prices and minimums remain vendor decisions; no access or license has been obtained by this research. ([CRS evaluation](https://crscreditapi.com/crs-sandbox-evaluation-credit-data-api/), [CRS partners](https://crscreditapi.com/company/partners), [Plaid sandbox](https://plaid.com/docs/sandbox/), [LoanPro trial](https://www.loanpro.io/faq/))

## CRS provides the first practical route

Submit the [CRS Get Started form](https://crscreditapi.com/get-started) and request both a **synthetic-data sandbox and platform-partner consultation**. CRS says sandbox evaluation can begin before signing and run alongside qualification. Its partner program covers platforms embedding data access and vendors adding data capabilities, with enterprise white labeling available. Ask CRS to choose the appropriate internal enterprise, platform or reseller arrangement for Ghost Guard’s actual users and report recipients; the public program does not itself grant redistribution rights. ([Sandbox process](https://crscreditapi.com/crs-sandbox-evaluation-credit-data-api/), [Partner program](https://crscreditapi.com/company/partners))

Prepare one packet containing the legal entity and website, a short description of the non-lender business model, intended users, internal versus customer-facing use, exact requested reports, expected volume, a data-flow diagram, consent/disclosure flow and security contact. Have proof of business and signer information ready; CRS publishes additional vetting requirements that vary by purpose. Request sample JSON/PDF output, enabled sandbox products, account-specific API documentation, production qualification steps, display/export/retention rights, and itemized pricing and minimums. This packet combines the information requested in CRS qualification, LexisNexis partner inquiries and bank-data onboarding. ([CRS requirements](https://crscreditapi.com/resources/compliance), [LexisNexis inquiry](https://risk.lexisnexis.com/about-us/alliance-partnerships/corporate), [Akoya onboarding](https://docs.akoya.com/guides/hub-manual-my-apps))

## Top Business requires its own entitlement confirmation

CRS’s public LexisNexis API reference documents **Business InstantID JSON and PDF** and says responses depend on the customer’s order configuration. Its catalog separately lists products including **Business Search Accurint Package Report and Enhanced Business Search**. These are evidence of distinct commercial products, not interchangeable report licenses. Do not treat an InstantID identity-verification integration, a general LexisNexis credential or a working sandbox call as proof that Ghost Guard can order the comprehensive report it calls “Top Business.” ([LexisNexis API reference](https://crscreditapi.redoc.ly/openapi/reference/tag/LexisNexis/), [CRS product catalog](https://crscreditapi.com/resources/product-catalog))

Ask CRS to confirm in writing: “What is the exact licensed product/SKU behind our requested Top Business report, is it enabled for our account, and what supported API or portal order flow produces it?” Obtain the required identifiers, sample report and authorized recipient/display/export rules before implementation. **No public source reviewed establishes a Top Business endpoint or Ghost Guard’s entitlement to it.** Direct LexisNexis documentation also distinguishes comprehensive business reports from access rights: SmartLinx access depends on subscription and administrative settings. ([CRS documentation](https://crscreditapi.redoc.ly/), [SmartLinx subscription boundary](https://amlinsight.lexisnexis.com/bps/web20_help/AML/bsp_report_smartlinx_business_r.html))

If CRS cannot supply the required report or distribution arrangement, approach the **LexisNexis Risk Solutions Corporate Alliance Program**, which explicitly includes resellers, developers and software integrators. Submit work contact details, company, location, industry, job title and solution of interest through its inquiry form, or call **1-800-953-2877**. Business Assurance Report is another direct enterprise due-diligence product, available through named portals or a representative; its public page does not promise an open API sandbox. ([Corporate alliances](https://risk.lexisnexis.com/about-us/alliance-partnerships/corporate), [Integrator inquiry](https://risk.lexisnexis.com/about-us/alliance-partnerships/corporate/integrators), [Business Assurance Report](https://risk.lexisnexis.com/products/business-assurance-report))

## Add bank data and workflow software through separate licenses

For **bank connectivity and financial data**, open the official [Plaid Dashboard](https://dashboard.plaid.com/) and use its free synthetic-data sandbox to evaluate account connections and API responses. This is the lowest-friction parallel evaluation; production data requires the relevant onboarding and commercial entitlement. If a different connectivity model fits better, Akoya provides a Data Recipient Hub and sandbox, Mastercard Open Finance U.S. provides a Test Drive, and MX offers a team-mediated API-key request. Akoya’s production process asks about use case, data consumption, users, volume, readiness and security controls, with provider approval also required. ([Plaid sandbox](https://plaid.com/docs/sandbox/), [Akoya access](https://docs.akoya.com/guides/hub-manual-gaining-access), [Akoya requirements](https://docs.akoya.com/guides/hub-manual-my-apps), [Mastercard official U.S. API repository](https://github.com/Mastercard/open-banking-us-openapi/blob/main/README.md), [MX API access](https://www.mx.com/api-access/))

For **origination, servicing and configurable financing workflows**, request a LoanPro trial with synthetic applications and state that Ghost Guard itself makes no loans. LoanPro publicly offers trial accounts plus product-integration, reseller and referral partner tracks, making it the clearest first commercial-software conversation. Ask sales whether Ghost Guard should use an internal enterprise evaluation or a partner agreement. LendAPI is the alternative: it advertises a free trial, API test mode and integrated-partner/reseller categories. Taktile provides a sales-led demo for decision workflows; the reviewed page does not establish a self-service sandbox. These products supply workflow capabilities, while CRS supplies the separately licensed reports. ([LoanPro trial](https://www.loanpro.io/faq/), [LoanPro partnerships](https://www.loanpro.io/partnerships), [LendAPI pricing](https://www.lendapi.com/pricing), [LendAPI test mode](https://developer.lendapi.com/reference/introduction), [LendAPI marketplace](https://www.lendapi.com/marketplace), [Taktile contact](https://taktile.com/contact))

## Make the first vendor request specific

Use this request with CRS: “Ghost Guard is a U.S. financing-services company and does not originate loans. We want to license business-report capabilities for our product. Please route us to your platform-partner or enterprise team and provide a synthetic-data sandbox. Our first target is the exact report identified as Top Business. Please confirm its product name/SKU, supported ordering API or portal, account entitlement, sample JSON/PDF, approved report recipients and display/export rights, production qualification requirements, and pricing/minimums.” The public CRS entry and partner routes support this request; final acceptance and terms remain unconfirmed. ([CRS Get Started](https://crscreditapi.com/get-started), [CRS partners](https://crscreditapi.com/company/partners))

Run qualification and synthetic evaluation together, then contract for the exact products before activating production use. If security testing is part of the evaluation, request a defined test tenant and written scope. A normal software license or API credential does not establish broader security-testing permission; MX, for example, publishes a separate vulnerability program with explicit account and method boundaries. ([CRS evaluation process](https://crscreditapi.com/crs-sandbox-evaluation-credit-data-api/), [MX vulnerability disclosure program](https://www.mx.com/vulnerability-disclosure-program/))

## Conclusion

The decisive procurement question is **which product Ghost Guard is entitled to use, for which users, through which supported interface**. Resolve that with CRS before committing engineering effort. A licensed CRS report product, a bank-data sandbox and a workflow-software trial can reproduce the needed capabilities through authorized vendor access; acquiring access to another company’s systems is unnecessary.
