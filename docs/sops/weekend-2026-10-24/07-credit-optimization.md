# SOP — Blueprint credit letters and optimization

November 2 candidate workflow. Release and authenticated buyer proof are recorded in the Capital Blueprint journey; this SOP alone is not deployment evidence.

The client works in their portal, under Letters and responses. Existing files also remain in What You Own. Their current credit job and mailing-proof upload remain in View progress.

## Prepare the right work

Read the saved current credit file and open waypoint before generating anything. The Blueprint funding saver stores inquiry-removal and personal-information letters when called for. A Metro 2 repair case has its own scope and sending rules; do not substitute that program or promise every buyer every type of dispute. After a genuine file update, regenerate only the work called for by the current analysis. Do not send an old batch again because a client uploaded another file.

## Client sends their own letters

1. Open Letters and responses and download each current letter. Check the client's identity, recipient and requested correction. If a fact is wrong or unclear, use Get help before sending.
2. Follow that letter's instructions. Sign/date where needed, include copies of requested records and retain a complete copy. Never send original identity or financial records.
3. Use the current recipient address in the instructions. Tracking or certified mail with return receipt can preserve delivery evidence.
4. In View progress, upload mailing proof. A report of mailing is not proof of bureau delivery or a changed credit file.
5. When a bureau replies, upload the actual response under Letters and responses, including all relevant pages.

Official instructions: [CFPB — disputing a credit-report error](https://www.consumerfinance.gov/ask-cfpb/how-do-i-dispute-an-error-on-my-credit-report-en-314/).

## Optional Fundhub mailing

Owner price: $97 USD for one selected mailing batch, November 2. The client selects the saved letters, reviews the return address and exact total, chooses a supported saved card or secure checkout, and explicitly authorizes this one payment and mailing. This is not a recurring mailing charge. No charge or mailing happens from opening the page or requesting a quote.

Only a confirmed paid order with frozen, current owned documents may proceed to mailing. Record provider acceptance and tracking separately from delivery. Unknown payment/send outcomes stay held for reconciliation; never charge or mail a second time merely because the browser timed out. No live charge or mail is authorized as a build test.

## Receive, validate and advance

The existing bureau-response reader handles uploads. Its saved verdict must bind the document and its current version, the correct client, readable bureau-response content and authenticity review. Unclear, mismatched, sample or apparently altered files stay open for another upload or team review. Do not mark the response step complete from upload existence alone.

Accepted document review is not proof that an account was corrected. Review the response and any required genuine credit update. A Blueprint response without an open repair case goes to the team; it does not create a fake repair case or automatic extra round. Advance the required waypoint from its actual evidence rule, then generate further letters only when the updated file calls for them.

FinanceOS is not live. Use current saved records, uploads, the existing document reader and supported credit updates; do not claim bank or credit outcomes were automatically verified.
