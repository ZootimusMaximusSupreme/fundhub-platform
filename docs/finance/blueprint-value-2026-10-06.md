## The real value

1. **The course sells knowledge. Blueprint sells decisions.** A course hands people 431 pages and hopes they figure it out. Blueprint reads their own file and tells them the one next move: which card, how much, what day, which bank, when to apply. They never have to learn the whole game.
2. **The loop is the moat.** Soft pull every 30 days → plan rewrites itself → steps clear only with proof → engine says "ready" → closer calls. No course can do that, because a course can't see the file.
3. **FinanceOS and Blueprint are one system with two doors.**
   - Blueprint makes the plan and runs the credit side: disputes, rounds, the closer.
   - FinanceOS watches the money every day: balances, due dates, card use, deposits.
   - Each covers the other's blind spot. The most common way a file gets wrecked between pulls is a missed payment, a high balance on statement day, or a new inquiry. FinanceOS catches those the day they happen.
4. **Upsell and downsell, same engine.**
   - Not ready for $5k–$10k? FinanceOS is the lower-priced door (setup fee + per container).
   - When the "ready to get funded" button fires inside FinanceOS, the CSM calls. That's the Blueprint upsell.
   - Blueprint buyers get FinanceOS as part of their plan.
   - Both read the same UnderwriteIQ engine and checklist, so nothing has to be built twice.

## Accountability is the product, not a bonus

From the research already in the repo (`docs/specs/W7-curriculum.md`):
- Self-paced courses finish 3–15% of the time (median 12.6%).
- Adding discussion and peers raises completion by about 23 points.
- A written "if-then" plan ("Tuesday at 9am I mail letter 2") has a medium-to-large effect on reaching the goal (d = .65 across 94 tests). Monthly goals don't.

**What that means:** information doesn't change what people do. A dated step with a reminder, a proof, and a person when they stall does.

How Blueprint + FinanceOS deliver that:
- Every step has a date: Plan tab pins.
- Every date gets a reminder: due-date texts, money-helper check-ins.
- Every step needs proof: the agent won't clear it without an upload.
- Silence goes to a person: CSM task on day 7 late, or when they ask.

**Quizzes:** use them where knowing matters ("which day do you pay before a round?"). For everything else, a proof upload beats a quiz. It shows they did it, not just that they read it.
