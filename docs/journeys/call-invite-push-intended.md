# Client call invite by push — intended journey (draft)

Role: a signed-in client who enabled Fundhub notifications on this iPhone. One job: tap a timely invite and join their current booked call. Push is an invitation to open the app, not an automatic or incoming phone call.

1. A real `booking.created` or `booking.rescheduled` starts the existing `ai-set-04-3way-handoff` run. It plans one wake 15 minutes before the call. A cancel or move stops the old run; a booking made inside 15 minutes, a past call, or an unreadable start time produces no 15-minute invite.
2. At wake, the run checks the saved booking again. The normal `SMS-AISET04-HANDOFF` text enters the existing message queue once per client and call time. Its usual opt-out, company-pause, quiet-hours, cap, draft, provider, and dry-run gates remain in charge. Push is additive; it never replaces the text or bypasses those gates.
3. Only after that text is actually marked `sent` by a transmitting provider may the sender consider one native call invite. It re-reads the matching current booking for the same company and client. The booking must still be `booked` or `rescheduled`, at the queued call time, and have a genuine HTTPS meeting URL. `/portal-login.html`, an empty value, a phone number, and any other fallback are **not** meeting links. The send window is from 15 minutes before the booked start until the start itself; a delayed text sent at or after the start causes no push.
4. An enabled, eligible iPhone gets one fixed, detail-free lock-screen alert: “Your call starts soon. Open Fundhub to join.” Its type is `call_invite`; its tap target is the existing client portal path. The payload contains no name, client or booking ID, meeting URL, clock time, phone number, or marketing copy. APNs expiry is the earlier of the booked end (when present) or 30 minutes after the start. The same client/call-time send key prevents a replay from making another invite. APNs failure does not undo or retry the successful text.
5. On tap, the native app opens its signed-in client view and fetches the booking **again** using that session. If signed out, it opens sign-in and reveals no call link. If the call moved, was cancelled, ended, or lost its link, it shows the current call state and no Join Call action. An old notification must never open an old link.
6. While the current call is joinable, a clear **Join Call** action opens its current HTTPS meeting URL after the client's tap. The app shows the destination host before opening it. The app does not dial, auto-join, start a microphone, or claim the other person has answered. If the meeting URL is absent, the call card shows time and confirmation state only; the existing text remains the fallback.
7. Browser push, marketing campaigns, and staff-triggered “call now” invitations are separate lanes. They may reuse the same authenticated call view later, but they do not gain a send path merely because this journey exists.

Proof before calling this live: a test for each gate and replay above; a real current-link and stale-link tap on an iPhone; an APNs delivery receipt **and** visible banner; then a client tap that opens the correct meeting destination. A push accepted by APNs without a visible or usable tap is not full proof.
