# Fundhub Prompter App Store — intended

## Owner decision

Fundhub Prompter and the Fundhub company app are separate iOS apps. Fundhub Prompter is to be submitted for public App Store distribution. The company app, not Prompter, gets the funnel link. This journey does not change either app's funnel.

## Role and access

The public can find and download Fundhub Prompter. The current native app is a staff filming tool: using it requires a Fundhub staff account. The listing must say so. Apple App Review must receive a working, non-expiring review account with an approved sample shoot; no password belongs in this repository.

## Use

1. Download Fundhub Prompter from its own public App Store listing and sign in with a Fundhub staff account.
2. See approved scripts in film order. Open a script and read it on the dark prompter screen.
3. Adjust the words and speed, use the on-screen controls or a paired remote, and optionally mirror words for a glass rig.
4. On a device with a suitable camera, allow camera and microphone access, record a take, and allow Photos add access to save it. This native release requests 1080p, with 60 fps attempted on the front camera and 30 fps fallback. The saved file's result requires a real-device check.
5. Edit a script paragraph when needed. The edit goes to Fundhub's script service; offline edits wait on the device and retry when the connection returns.
6. Open the privacy policy from sign-in or Settings. Contact Fundhub through the support link on the App Store listing.

## States and release proof

- No staff account: sign-in remains visible; the app does not offer a customer portal or public script library.
- No approved shoot: the script list explains that scripts need approval in the dashboard.
- No camera or Photos access: the app shows the problem. Do not call a take saved unless it appears in Photos.
- No network: cached scripts may show; edits remain queued on the device.
- App Review: use a new build after the startup crash fix, with a working review login and sample scripts. Verify a real iPhone preview and saved original before describing recording quality as proved.
- Public release: complete listing, screenshots, privacy answers, pricing, availability, age rating, review information, and submission in App Store Connect. Verify Apple's approval and the public listing before sharing a store URL.

The current TestFlight build 1 remains untouched. Changes in this track target build 2 and do not mean it was uploaded, submitted, approved, or published.
