# Teleprompter video repair rules

These rules apply to every change to `public/app/teleprompter.*` or its recording and Drive upload path. Read them with `AGENTS.md` and the shoot-flow specs.

**Owner call (2026-10-13): every take is a hard 30 fps until the iPhone app.** The locked features are in `.ai/rules/teleprompter-camera.md`. Where this page says 60 fps, that is the target for the iPhone app, not for Safari.

Read the actual track settings again once the preview is ready and at recording start/end. A known granted rate below 30 fps must show a failed quality warning, even when the resolution is correct. The request's ideal rate must never stand in for the granted rate.

**Screen guardrail (owner, 2026-10-12):** change the filming screen only when the owner asks for that specific screen work. Use Sol for requested screen changes and Luna for recording/settings checks. Sol and Luna are Claude agents (owner-set 2026-10-24), so this does not clash with `grok-no-displays`: only Claude changes what people see. Preserve the camera view, word size, reading line, and controls during recording repairs. The badge shows granted camera settings; saved-file frame rate is verified separately. Never fill the badge from the selected preset or claim 60 fps when the camera reports a lower or unknown rate.

1. **Name the measured failure first.** Record the exact take, time, device orientation, preview appearance, raw Drive file dimensions, distinct frames per second, and frame gaps. A requested camera setting is not a measured result.
2. **Locate the failing stage before calling a fix.** Check, in order, the camera frame shown by Safari, frames copied to the recording canvas, the locally created file, the raw file downloaded from Drive, and the upload code. If a stage has no evidence, add a small measurement and say the cause is still open. Preserve raw recording counts and granted camera settings with each saved take, through the device queue and into its Drive description, so the agent can read them without asking the owner to transcribe a temporary status line. Store only bounded recording measurements; omit device IDs, microphone labels, tokens, and script content. Do not guess that Drive, Safari, or the encoder is at fault.
3. **Keep the owner's capture targets.** Law: teleprompter-camera (.ai/rules/teleprompter-camera.md) - every take is a hard 30 fps; ads 1080p, other videos 4K (testimonial card videos on pages excepted); front camera saved mirrored. These are saved-file acceptance targets, not just camera requests. A granted rate below 30 fps remains a failed quality proof; never call it ready or pad a file with duplicate frames to claim a higher rate. The upright preview and saved video must show an upright, usable portrait picture with the full camera view: no crop, stretched image, thin letterboxed strip, or unexplained black bars. Apply the same rule sideways in landscape.
4. **Change one cause at a time.** Preserve the working camera flip, smooth word scroll, one-file recording, and device-first save. Do not change a camera request merely because it looks plausible in desktop tests; check the dimensions Safari actually gives on the iPhone. If a new request breaks the preview, repair that request before changing upload or scroll code.
5. **Prove each stage before asking for another owner test.** Run focused code and browser checks, verify the new script is live, then check the preview on the real iPhone before recording. Use one short take to check the preview, saved file, sound, and frame rate. Decode the uploaded original frame by frame; Drive preview smoothness alone is not proof. Never call it ready to film while the real-phone proof is missing or red.
6. **Report evidence and the next action plainly.** Say what is confirmed, what remains unknown, and the one next check. Never say “fixed,” “smooth,” or “live” from a green test or a requested frame rate alone.

7. **Keep fresh camera frames within the recording budget.** Each video-frame callback supplies a fresh frame. Do not discard it because the callback arrives 1 ms early when the recording budget can keep the source rate. Use advancing presentation timestamps when reducing a faster source for the 4K recording budget. A live Safari stream may keep `mediaTime` at zero; never depend on that clock advancing. Count `presentedFrames` as well as callbacks so missed callbacks cannot be mistaken for a slow camera.
8. **Check the real encoder after changing recording.** A mocked recorder cannot prove playable output or saved frame cadence. Run a real WebKit recording and decode its output before release. Compare copied frames with encoded frames, and test fixed live timestamps and missing optional canvas `requestFrame()` support. Where supported, request capture after painting instead of relying only on a separate timer to notice the new frame.

## Acceptance checklist (moved here from AGENTS.md)

- The front camera must open to a usable, slightly soft preview. Switching front/back must not start, stop, or split the recording. The Record button controls starts and manual stops; reaching the script end adds an exact two-second tail before automatic stop.
- Ads use full-frame 1080p at a hard 30 fps. VSLs, thank-you videos, and testimonials use full-frame 4K at a hard 30 fps. 60 fps waits for the iPhone app (owner call 2026-10-13). Testimonial card videos on pages do not need 4K (owner-set 2026-10-24). Never infer the saved frame rate from the request; measure the original Drive video before claiming smooth filming.
- When a take is accepted, save it on the device first, then queue it for upload. Filming the next take must not pause or delete older queued takes. Reopening the app must recover saved queued takes. Never promise that an unfinished iPhone browser recording survives force-closing the app.
- Show the mic label from `teleprompter-camera` ("iPhone mic on" or "Rode on") if the browser provides it, with small live audio levels. Show descriptive names from `marketing/ads/NAMING.md`. Use the canonical offer key and funnel tag to send the take to the right marketing offer's Drive folder, including new registered offers. The lower Drive icon opens the current offer's folder on a double-tap.
- Prove the core flows in automated tests and check the shipped site on a real iPhone before calling the teleprompter ready to film. If the owner will deploy manually, leave a reviewable GitHub PR and precise manual steps.
- Law: slo-one-filmed-folder (.ai/rules/slo-one-filmed-folder.md) - Direct Book recordings never go in the SLO folder.
